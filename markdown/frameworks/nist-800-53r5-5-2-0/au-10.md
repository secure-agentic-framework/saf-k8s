# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - AU-10

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Non-repudiation

## Mapping Notes

Where the organization defines the keyless image-signing action as an action requiring non-repudiation and binds the workload signer identity, signed digest, and verifiable log evidence to that action. The fixture provides evidence of one service-account signing event; it does not attribute an individual, cover other agent actions, or establish enterprise non-repudiation policy.
