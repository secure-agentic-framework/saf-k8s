- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Use of All-source Intelligence

## Mapping Notes

The classification and pattern library can contribute threat information to risk analysis when populated with relevant documented intelligence. RA-3(2) requires the organization to use all-source intelligence in its risk assessment.
