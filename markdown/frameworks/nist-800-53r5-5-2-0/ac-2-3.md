# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - AC-2(3)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Disable Accounts

## Mapping Notes

The approved workflow disables or removes identified inactive system accounts. Verify the adopted thresholds, history completeness and the effective retirement outcome.
