# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - AC-6(7)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Review of User Privileges

## Mapping Notes

Periodic Kubernetes RBAC configuration audits supply evidence for reviewing assigned privileges. The organization defines the review frequency and reassigns or removes privileges whose need cannot be confirmed.
