- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Self-reliant Trustworthiness

## Mapping Notes

Denying sensitive use on absent evidence can contribute to secure behavior when an external verifier is unavailable. Evaluate the defined element's dependence on the verifier and its behavior in isolation. A remote appraisal result by itself supplies evidence of measured state.
