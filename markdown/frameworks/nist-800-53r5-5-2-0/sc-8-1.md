# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - SC-8(1)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Cryptographic Protection

## Mapping Notes

The selection process may support cryptographic transmission protection by rejecting candidates that do not satisfy the approved encryption criterion. Separate case SAF-K8S-0502-002 demonstrates one Cilium 1.20.5 WireGuard path with a fixed marker and status/peer/counter evidence. The selection case itself tests no cryptographic data path and does not establish production coverage.
