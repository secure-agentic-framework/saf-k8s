## Mapping Notes

The native fixture demonstrates a promotion decision that rejects failed, skipped, or missing required checks and rejects mismatched artifact digest, source revision, test revision, or policy revision, then admits and reads back one matching immutable record. This supports a pre-finalization check gate; it does not demonstrate change approval, test execution, the validity of evidence producers, or the organization’s change records.
