# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - CP-4(4)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Full Recovery and Reconstitution

## Mapping Notes

The case tests a single digest-pinned mock Deployment's transition from available to contained and back to available. It does not restore etcd, perform full system recovery or reconstitution, validate model operation, or verify the complete system's known state. Retain only as a component-level test contribution when the adopter records the complete full-recovery exercise and separate verification of all other components and mission functions.
