# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - AU-9(4)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Access by Subset of Privileged Users

## Mapping Notes

A Kubernetes RBAC audit can identify grants to manage Kubernetes-backed audit logging and compare the holders of those grants with the approved subset of privileged users or roles. Evidence of effective restrictions and the approved subset comes from the audit-logging authorization controls.
