# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - AU-10(2)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Validate Binding of Information Producer Identity

## Mapping Notes

Where the organization defines image-signature validation frequency and validation-error actions for the covered release path. The fixture verifies signer identity/digest before its release step and rejects wrong identity and issuer; it does not establish frequency or response actions for all releases.
