# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - CM-2(3)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Retention of Previous Configurations

## Mapping Notes

The fixture sets a Deployment revision history limit, retains the prior immutable model artifact, and verifies native rollback restores the prior release, digest, and served predictions. It does not establish a durable organization-wide retention policy or archival period.
