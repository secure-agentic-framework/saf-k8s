# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - SC-28

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Protection of Information at Rest

## Mapping Notes

Storage-layer encryption protects the confidentiality of organization-defined etcd data and WAL at rest on governed media. Assess effective device and snapshot coverage and key custody for each deployment.
