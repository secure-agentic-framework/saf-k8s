# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - SC-7(11)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Restrict Incoming Communications Traffic

## Mapping Notes

The ingress-only phase denies the unapproved owned listener while unrestricted egress is preserved; the approved client-to-listener route remains available. The proof covers one synthetic in-cluster source/destination pair only.
