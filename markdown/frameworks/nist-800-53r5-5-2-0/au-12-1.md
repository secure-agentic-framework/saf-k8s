# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - AU-12(1)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: System-wide and Time-correlated Audit Trail

## Mapping Notes

The fixture confirms equality of source timestamps in the selected Zot audit/access records and the received corpus, but does not test a multi-component clock-tolerance bound or establish system-wide coverage. The organization-defined component set and time-correlation tolerance remain adopter requirements.
