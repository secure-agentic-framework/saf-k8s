# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - CP-4

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Contingency Plan Testing

## Mapping Notes

The case is a repeatable technical exercise of synthetic workload containment and gated recovery. It does not test an approved organizational contingency plan at its defined frequency, establish readiness of personnel, include organizational operational effects, or show officials reviewing results and initiating corrective actions. Count it only as one test component when those adopter program requirements are evidenced.
