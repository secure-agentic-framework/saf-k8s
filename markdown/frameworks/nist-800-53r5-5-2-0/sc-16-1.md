# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - SC-16(1)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Integrity Verification

## Mapping Notes

Where signer identity and digest are designated security attributes associated with the transmitted image. The fixture verifies those attributes through the signature/certificate and transparency material for one OCI digest; it does not cover other transmitted security/privacy attributes.
