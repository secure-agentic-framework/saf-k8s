# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - AC-4(21)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Physical or Logical Separation of Information Flows

## Mapping Notes

The case independently exercises ingress and egress policy isolation and then permits a single scoped path. This can support separation of those selected communication flows, but it does not separate information by classification or sensitivity labels.
