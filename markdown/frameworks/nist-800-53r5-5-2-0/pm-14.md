# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - PM-14

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Testing, Training, and Monitoring

## Mapping Notes

The synthetic case supplies one repeatable technical incident-response exercise. It does not establish or maintain organizational testing, training, and monitoring plans, ensure their ongoing execution, provide training, or monitor deployed systems. Count it only as a test component of the broader PM-14 process when the adopter supplies the plans, recurring execution records, strategy-alignment review, and separate training and monitoring evidence.
