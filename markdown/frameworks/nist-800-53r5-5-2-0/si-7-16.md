# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - SI-7(16)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Time Limit on Process Execution Without Supervision

## Mapping Notes

The fixture verifies Kubernetes Job activeDeadlineSeconds terminates a Ready, inert Job after its configured 20-second active deadline. This supports a bounded process-duration safeguard only; it does not establish supervision policy, broader execution authorization, software integrity, or production job recovery.
