- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Establish and Maintain Trust Relationship with Providers

## Mapping Notes

The control can contribute to deciding whether an external AI service is approved for use, but its portable fixture does not establish or maintain provider trust, contractual terms, identity, or service assurance.
