# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - SC-5(2)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Capacity, Bandwidth, and Redundancy

## Mapping Notes

The fixture demonstrates a 16 MiB namespace storage-request quota and per-container memory ceilings; these are bounded resource allocation measures relevant to selected capacity safeguards. It does not verify excess compute headroom, bandwidth, redundancy, or operations during a contingency.
