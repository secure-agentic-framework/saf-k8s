# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - SA-8(18)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Trusted Communications Channels

## Mapping Notes

The bounded reference observes one verified worker-to-aggregator TLS connection with authenticated admission and refuses an incorrect Service DNS name. Verify the architecture, credential lifecycle and all required aggregation or all-reduce channels in the adopted environment.
