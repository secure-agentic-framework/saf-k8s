# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - SR-11

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Component Authenticity

## Mapping Notes

Where approved signer identities and verified signatures are adopted as one technical measure in the organization's anti-counterfeit policy for the covered software components. The fixture verifies one image signature and signer; it does not establish anti-counterfeit procedures, source-vendor authenticity, reporting, or counterfeit handling.
