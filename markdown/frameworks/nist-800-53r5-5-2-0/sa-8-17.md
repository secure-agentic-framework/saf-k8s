# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - SA-8(17)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Secure Distributed Composition

## Mapping Notes

NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5.2.0 SA-8(17) requires implementing secure distributed composition as an organization-defined engineering principle. The scoped contribution is limited to architecture-specific aggregation policy composition when that exact distributed design is documented and assessed; it is not evidence of allreduce correctness or Byzantine resilience.
