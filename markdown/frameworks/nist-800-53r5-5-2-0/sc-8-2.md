- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Pre- and Post-transmission Handling

## Mapping Notes

NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5.2.0 SC-8(2) addresses maintaining confidentiality or integrity during information preparation for transmission and reception; its discussion includes aggregation and protocol transformation points. The relation is limited to concrete update-integrity validation at those defined boundaries and requires separate evidence of the selected property.
