# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - CM-2(6)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Development and Test Environments

## Mapping Notes

The fixture demonstrates a namespace-scoped separation between one synthetic training input and one production inference reference, including own-reference RBAC and admission denial of the known training ConfigMap. It does not establish configuration management, testing isolation, or separation of every development/test environment from operations.
