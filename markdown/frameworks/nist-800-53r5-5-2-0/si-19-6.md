# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - SI-19(6)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Differential Privacy

## Mapping Notes

NIST SI-19(6) is specifically a de-identification enhancement to prevent disclosure of PII by adding non-deterministic noise before results are reported. SAF-K8S-0901-005 explicitly requires differential privacy for gradient updates, so this is a bounded conceptual contribution, not blanket equivalence or proof of an accountant, parameter selection, composition, or privacy guarantee.
