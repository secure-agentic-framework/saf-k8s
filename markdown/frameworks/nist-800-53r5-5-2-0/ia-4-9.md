# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - IA-4(9)

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Attribute Maintenance and Protection

## Mapping Notes

The reference case maintains two synthetic identity attributes in a case-owned registry and demonstrates narrow registrar RBAC, admission denial of unapproved changes and removals, approved-value readback, and RequestResponse audit. This supports protection and maintenance of selected Kubernetes service identity attributes only. The namespaced fixture registry is not an organization-wide central store and does not prove that a cloud federation provider, NetworkPolicy, or other consumer maps or honors the attribute; establish those bindings and coverage separately.
