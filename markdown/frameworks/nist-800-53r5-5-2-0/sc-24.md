# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - SC-24

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Fail in Known State

## Mapping Notes

Gatekeeper can support the admission-layer portion of SC-24 when an organization designates rejecting matching writes during a Gatekeeper invocation failure as the known state and verifies preservation of its defined system-state information for that failure.
