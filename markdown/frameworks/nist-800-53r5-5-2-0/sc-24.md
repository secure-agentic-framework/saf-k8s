# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0 - SC-24

- Framework Code: NIST-800-53R5
- Requirement Title: Fail in Known State

## Mapping Notes

The case observes fail-closed admission when the external image-verification provider is absent and successful admission after provider recovery. It establishes that one unverified Pod is rejected under that failure condition; it does not establish the organization-defined known state or preservation of system-state information required for broader SC-24 coverage.
