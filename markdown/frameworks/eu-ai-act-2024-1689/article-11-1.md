# EU AI Act 2024/1689 - Article-11.1

- Framework Code: EU-AI-ACT
- Requirement Title: Technical documentation

## Mapping Notes

The SBOM can supply a maintained inventory for one documented release image. Verify completeness and maintenance of the required technical documentation independently.
