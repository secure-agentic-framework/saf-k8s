# EU AI Act 2024/1689 - Article-72.1

- Framework Code: EU-AI-ACT
- Requirement Title: Post-market monitoring by providers and post-market monitoring plan for high-risk AI systems

## Mapping Notes

This tracing control may be one technical component of a provider-established and documented post-market monitoring system only when its deployed coverage and signal selection are proportionate to the actual AI technology and risks and are included in the provider's system description. The reference is a synthetic collector path; it does not establish or document the provider's monitoring system.
