# EU AI Act 2024/1689 - Annex-VII.3.2

- Framework Code: EU-AI-ACT
- Requirement Title: Conformity based on an assessment of the quality management system and an assessment of the technical documentation

## Mapping Notes

Scoped Kubernetes artifacts may contribute provider evidence for the quality management assessment. The notified body performs the assessment and issues its decision.
