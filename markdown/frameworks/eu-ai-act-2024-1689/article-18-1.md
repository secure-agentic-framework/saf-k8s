# EU AI Act 2024/1689 - Article-18.1

- Framework Code: EU-AI-ACT
- Requirement Title: Documentation keeping

## Mapping Notes

Registry retention and hold evidence can support custody of the specific documents governed by Article 18(1) if their versions are identified and the required duration and competent-authority availability are verified. The test demonstrates scoped artifact retention and garbage collection. Applicable documentation scope, statutory retention and availability require separate evidence.
