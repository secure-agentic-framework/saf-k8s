# NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 - MEASURE 3.2

- Framework Code: NIST-AI-RMF
- Requirement Title: Risk tracking approaches are considered for settings where AI risks are difficult to assess using currently available measurement techniques or where metrics are not yet available.

## Mapping Notes

Periodic evidence-linked OCTAVE reviews may support tracking of relevant hard-to-measure platform risks.
