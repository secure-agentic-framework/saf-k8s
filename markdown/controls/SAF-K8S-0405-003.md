# SAF-K8S-0405-003 - Kubeconfig security and hygiene

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.5 - Identity Abuse Detection and Mitigation
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Manage kubeconfigs and their referenced credentials under approved storage, endpoint trust, identity and authorization requirements. Restrict intended contexts and credential mechanisms, verify actual credential lifetime and retirement, and audit access and local exposure.

## Objective

Limit misuse of exposed, stale or unapproved kubeconfig access paths while preserving explicitly approved operation.

## Rationale

Kubeconfig contains routing and authentication configuration; lifetime and revocation depend on the effective credential and actual verifier. File permissions, encryption, trusted plugins, identity verification and authorization protect separate parts of that path.

## Scope

Applies to governed human, CI/CD and automation kubeconfig files and referenced trust and credentials. Record approved use cases, endpoints, custody, plugin policy and exposure handling.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Approved client and endpoint configuration, host or runner policy, and independently verified authentication and authorization at the accepting platform.
- Implementation Surface: Inventory effective profiles and contexts, trust sources and credential mechanisms. Test file access, endpoint trust, approved identity, actual least privilege, issuer lifetime and offboarding. Verify storage encryption and trusted plugin execution where applicable.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Developer Experience
