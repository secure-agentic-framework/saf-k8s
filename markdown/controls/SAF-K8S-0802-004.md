# SAF-K8S-0802-004 - GPU firmware integrity monitoring

- Domain: D08 - GPU, Accelerator, and Confidential Computing
- Knowledge Area: 8.2 - GPU Driver, Library, and Toolkit Security
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Inventory accelerator firmware and verify the trust and change-evidence mechanisms supported by the exact platform. Correlate observed versions with approved change records, alert on unexplained drift and document unsupported integrity measurements and compensating controls.

## Objective

Detect unauthorized or unexplained accelerator platform changes and retain appropriately scoped firmware integrity and operational evidence.

## Rationale

Reported firmware versions, checksums, health telemetry, signature checks and attestation establish different properties. Compare trusted observations with an approved platform baseline and change windows. Treat operational anomalies as indicators for review.

## Scope

Applies to governed accelerator nodes and provider-managed hardware. Inventory exact device and firmware scope, supported observation or attestation path, approved windows, signal limitations and documented exceptions.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Approved firmware inventory, platform-supported measurements and authentication, reconciled change records and alert review.
- Implementation Surface: Record independently obtained firmware versions and applicable supported trust results. Verify change authorization and identify missing or mismatched measurements. Distinguish InfoROM checksums and health telemetry from applicable signed measurements or attestation. Retain alert routing, reviewer decisions and ownership for unsupported properties.
- Owner Primary: GPU Infrastructure Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering
