# SAF-K8S-0908-009 - Differential privacy parameter governance for inference endpoints

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.8 - Model Abuse and Extraction Prevention
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Before deploying a formal differential privacy claim, approve and document the actual mechanism, privacy unit and neighboring relation, parameters, data boundary and intended utility. Verify that the deployed implementation and its configuration match the reviewed guarantee.

## Objective

Ensure each formal differential privacy claim applies to a defined mechanism and protected population under reviewed assumptions.

## Rationale

Numerical privacy parameters describe a guarantee only together with the mechanism, protected unit and implementation assumptions. A review record is necessary governance evidence, while correctness and utility require their own verification.

## Scope

Applies to governed inference and release systems that claim formal differential privacy. Identify the relevant training, input or release mechanism and applicable guarantee. Verify additional cumulative releases under SAF-K8S-0908-010 where they consume privacy budget.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: The actual deployed mechanism and approved configuration and review at its serving boundary.
- Implementation Surface: Verify the approved mechanism and implementation version, privacy unit and neighboring relation, exact or bounded parameters, applicable trust and utility assumptions, conformance evidence and fail-closed handling of unapproved settings.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; Privacy Engineering
