# SAF-K8S-0405-020 - Break-glass recovery exercise validation

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.5 - Identity Abuse Detection and Mitigation
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Exercise each approved emergency access procedure at its defined cadence using realistic and authorized failure scenarios. Verify that independently provisioned recovery access reaches the intended cluster operation when the relevant normal dependency is unavailable. Verify restoration and withdrawal of temporary access.

## Objective

Demonstrate that authorized operators can restore defined Kubernetes capabilities during the covered failure and retire emergency access afterward within the approved objectives.

## Rationale

An emergency path may share a failed dependency or contain stale authorization, runbook steps or oversight. Configuration review and the presence of a credential do not establish the resulting recovery behavior. A failed new identity-provider login can differ from validation of a previously issued credential.

## Scope

Applies to the real normal and emergency access paths for each governed cluster. Inventory failure modes, dependent issuers, authorized and independent recovery scope, approval and evidence, safe test limits, after-action review and removal of temporary access.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Observed execution of the authorized recovery exercise through the defined normal and fallback paths.
- Implementation Surface: Observe the intended normal operation, exercise the selected dependency failure, authenticate and authorize the designated recovery operation, verify effective scope and actual outcomes, restore normal access and withdraw emergency grants. Retain provider and API evidence, timing, unresolved differences and accountable follow-up.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Identity and Access Management
