# SAF-K8S-0604-007 - Third-party component security requirements documentation

- Domain: D06 - Supply Chain, Images, and Admission Control
- Knowledge Area: 6.4 - SBOMs and Vulnerability Intelligence
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Document and version the security criteria for governed third-party intake. Communicate applicable requirements through the organization's approved supplier channels and retain the communication and review record. Assess risk-appropriate disclosure, support, update, licensing, integrity and component inventory evidence. Require approved, artifact-specific results before deployed components enter the governed Kubernetes path.

## Objective

Link supplier communication and approval to the active requirements, and enforce the technical conditions that can be checked for an exact approved release.

## Rationale

SSDF v1.1 PO.1.3 addresses communicating requirements to suppliers of commercial software components intended for reuse in the organization's own software. The organization can apply broader intake policy to open-source, operational or AI artifacts. Collect supplier and acquisition evidence independently from Kubernetes enforcement.

## Scope

All third-party software components consumed by the Kubernetes platform and AI workloads: container images, Helm charts, operators, CSI/CNI plugins, ML frameworks, pre-trained models, and pipeline components.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Supplier intake or procurement for the applicable review and communication. Release or admission for the verified artifact and review binding.
- Implementation Surface: Maintain versioned intake criteria, communication and review evidence, and authorized exception decisions. Verify technical evidence for the exact component and release, such as supported identity, signature and inventory checks where required. Restrict who can publish machine-readable approved results, and test the governed deployment path against missing, obsolete or mismatched evidence.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; ML Platform Engineering; Procurement
