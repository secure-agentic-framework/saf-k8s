# SAF-K8S-0102-001 - etcd storage-layer disk encryption with externally managed keys

- Domain: D01 - Control Plane and Cluster Hardening
- Knowledge Area: 1.2 - etcd and Cluster State Protection
- Maturity: Foundational
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Protect governed etcd data and WAL backing media with storage-layer encryption under access-controlled key custody separate from protected media. Verify covered paths and the applicable infrastructure or provider key-management boundary.

## Objective

Protect offline etcd data at its storage boundary independently of Kubernetes API-resource encryption.

## Rationale

API-object encryption applies to configured resource values and does not encrypt the entire underlying etcd database, WAL or backing media. Media encryption addresses offline exposure. Key custody, authorized access to running systems and protection of separate backup copies require their own evidence.

## Scope

Applies to governed production and sensitive non-production clusters. Inventory the etcd data and WAL paths, backing devices, protected infrastructure snapshots and customer or provider key responsibilities. Assess external backup copies and API-object encryption separately.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Effective node or provider storage encryption and protected key custody for every identified backing device or image.
- Implementation Surface: Record the media type, actual paths, encrypted device or image, scope of covered snapshots, key custodian, authorized unlock, and recovery evidence. For managed infrastructure obtain applicable provider evidence. Do not use an API-resource encryption configuration as the media proof.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; Key Management/Cryptography; Cloud Platform Engineering
