# SAF-K8S-0604-004 - VEX (Vulnerability Exploitability eXchange) publication

- Domain: D06 - Supply Chain, Images, and Admission Control
- Knowledge Area: 6.4 - SBOMs and Vulnerability Intelligence
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Publish and consume authenticated VEX assessments for governed releases where applicable. Record the format and version, issuer, document revision, precise product and component identity, advisory and format-specific status. Verify evidence and exact release scope before changing prioritization, and retain the original finding and decision.

## Objective

Use reviewed and current product-specific assessments to prioritize vulnerability work while preserving actionable or unresolved findings.

## Rationale

A VEX statement communicates an issuer's assessment for identified products and a point in time. Verify the source, scope and evidence independently. OpenVEX states are affected, not_affected, fixed, and under_investigation. CycloneDX and CSAF use their own defined representations.

## Scope

Applies to approved vulnerability assessment workflows for governed container image releases and relevant third-party assessments. Inventory each configured consumer, source and format version. Define review authority and accepted transport, evidence and retention.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Vulnerability management intake verifies the source, release, advisory and component before using an assessment. Preserve the active finding when evidence is unavailable, conflicting or mismatched.
- Implementation Surface: Pin and test each format and consumer. For OpenVEX require a justification or impact statement for not_affected and an action statement for affected. Keep under_investigation open and verify reported fixes against the selected release and the applicable remediation policy. Check issuer trust, exact product and package binding, revision and freshness, and record input reports and decisions. Test deployment and vendor integrations independently.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: DevSecOps / Release Engineering; ML Platform Engineering
