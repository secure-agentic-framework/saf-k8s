# SAF-K8S-0704-005 - Restricted use policies for non-organizationally owned systems and external AI services

- Domain: D07 - Storage, Multi-tenancy, and Resource Governance
- Knowledge Area: 7.4 - Cloud Provider Security Integration
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Define and enforce the conditions under which Kubernetes workloads may use external systems or AI services. Approve specific services, purposes, information classes and authorized workloads or users. Apply verified admission and egress controls to the decisions they can enforce and retain independent approval and operating evidence.

## Objective

Protect organizational information by restricting external service use to approved workloads, purposes and data classes.

## Rationale

Organizational approval, Kubernetes object admission and runtime egress govern different decisions. A verified egress policy can constrain supported connections. Contractual obligations, information use, identity and payload handling require controls and evidence appropriate to their own boundaries.

## Scope

Applies to governed workloads with access to external services. Inventory services, purposes, identities, protected information classes, provider requirements, egress paths and exceptions.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Organizational risk and service approval, workload admission policy, and the deployed network or application egress boundary.
- Implementation Surface: Record provider, information and identity approvals. Enforce the approved scope with available admission, name or endpoint identity, and egress mechanisms. Verify CNI or gateway enforcement with controlled allowed and denied observations. Govern credentials and logging separately and review relevant crosswalks against their exact requirements.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Cloud Platform Engineering; Platform Engineering; Legal; Procurement
