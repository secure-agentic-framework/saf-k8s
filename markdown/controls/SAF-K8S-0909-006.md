# SAF-K8S-0909-006 - Vector index lifecycle management

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.9 - RAG Infrastructure Security
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Maintain versioned recoverable snapshots, observed integrity checks, controlled rebuild and migration procedures, and verified retirement of governed vector indexes and their retained copies.

## Objective

Preserve recoverable and approved vector index state and verify retirement using the applicable data and storage policy.

## Rationale

Structural storage checks alone do not establish approved index contents or correct retrieval. Model changes require a compatibility assessment and may require a controlled rebuild. Logical deletion alone does not establish sanitization of storage or retained copies.

## Scope

Applies to vector index snapshots, approved contents and retrieval semantics, versioned rebuild and migration, and retirement of primary and retained copies. Coordinate general platform backup and incident recovery processes.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: The index storage and staging lifecycle, activation checks, and the applicable storage and retention authorities.
- Implementation Surface: Verify an approved manifest, representative known queries, successful recovery and lifecycle state transitions. Assess schema and model compatibility before migration. Apply and verify storage specific retirement and sanitization for applicable volumes, snapshots, replicas and backups.
- Owner Primary: ML Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Infrastructure Engineering; Security Engineering
