# SAF-K8S-0601-018 - Registry push-time container image vulnerability rescanning

- Domain: D06 - Supply Chain, Images, and Admission Control
- Knowledge Area: 6.1 - Container Image and Registry Security
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Rescan each governed image after registry admission and refresh the evidence as advisory coverage or policy changes. Require a current disposition bound to the exact registry manifest digest before promotion or deployment.

## Objective

Keep deployed and promoted images subject to current post-push vulnerability evidence for their actual manifest and platform.

## Rationale

Build checks may precede later disclosures or miss imported images. Result age, scanner and feed identity, digest matching and alternate deployment paths need independent controls.

## Scope

Applies to governed registry manifests and promotion or admission. Exercise real import and push events, refresh cadence, multi-architecture coverage and quarantine. Assess vulnerability exposure for already running workloads separately.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Registry-side scan and rescan orchestration, trusted disposition storage and promotion or deployment policy.
- Implementation Surface: Record digest and platform, scanner and advisory revisions and clock. Treat missing, stale or failed evidence as ineligible. Reassess on feed changes, retain decisions and apply approved exceptions to exact artifacts and time windows.
- Owner Primary: DevSecOps / Release Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; Security Engineering
