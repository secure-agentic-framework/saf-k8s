# SAF-K8S-1007-007 - Cluster service protection from AI training resource exhaustion

- Domain: D10 - Observability, Incident Response, and Governance
- Knowledge Area: 10.7 - Cluster Lifecycle and Asset Inventory
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Organizations must protect shared cluster services such as DNS, metrics and logging during large-scale training. Set and verify training namespace quotas for accounted resource requests and limits, configure appropriate service and workload scheduling priorities, and monitor service health against defined objectives. Verify that provisioned capacity and placement leave the required headroom across the applicable resource paths.

## Objective

Ensure that shared cluster services remain available despite resource-intensive AI workload demands.

## Rationale

Large-scale training jobs that consume excessive CPU, memory, or network bandwidth can starve cluster-level services (DNS, metrics collection, log forwarding), creating observability blind spots that adversaries can exploit to operate undetected.

## Scope

Applies to Kubernetes clusters where large-scale training jobs run alongside shared cluster services. Covers resource quota enforcement on training namespaces and cluster service health monitoring during training job execution.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Resource quota enforcement for training namespaces and PriorityClass configuration for cluster services.
- Implementation Surface: Enforce training namespace admission budgets with ResourceQuota and independently verify service requests, node allocatable capacity, placement, and protected capacity. Configure appropriate PriorityClasses and preemption policies, and restrict built-in system critical priorities to their intended system components. Validate DNS resolution and latency, metrics scrape success and freshness, and log delivery and lag against defined objectives under representative workload conditions.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: SRE; AI/ML Engineering
