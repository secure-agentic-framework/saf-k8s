# SAF-K8S-0402-003 - Service account authenticator and identifier exposure protection

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.2 - Service Accounts and Workload Identity
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Prevent disclosure of service account bearer tokens and other service account authenticators through public repositories, container images, application logs, errors, or externally accessible API responses. Minimize public disclosure of service account names and other identifiers when they reveal sensitive account or workload structure. Retain authorized operational and audit identity fields where required.

## Objective

Protect service account authenticators from disclosure and minimize exposure of sensitive account identifiers where policy requires.

## Rationale

A valid bearer token can authenticate its holder to the configured relying party. Exposed identifiers can disclose workload relationships or account structure. Apply secret protection to authenticator values and keep account identifiers available to authorized operational and audit systems.

## Scope

Applies to service account credentials and sensitive identifiers in governed repositories and history, CI artifacts and logs, container image layers, application and retained logs, external API responses and documentation.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Credential scanning and controlled redaction at repository, build, application response, and log boundaries.
- Implementation Surface: Use approved scanners for service account authenticator forms and independently verify current and historical repository, CI, image and log coverage. Minimize credential values before retention and public responses. Apply a distinct policy for sensitive identifiers, preserve required audit fields and exercise inert canary comparisons on all governed paths.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Application Development; Platform Engineering
