# SAF-K8S-0903-001 - Adversarial example defenses at the serving layer

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.3 - Inference Resilience, Adversarial Defense, and Resource Controls
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Inference serving owners must select and verify serving-layer input safeguards appropriate to the model, input modalities, endpoint exposure, and documented threats. Define and enforce the disposition of invalid or policy-rejected inputs and the behavior when a required safeguard is unavailable. Where explanation output is provided, authorize it separately from ordinary inference, limit its disclosure according to the assessed risk, and verify any configured disclosure limits.

## Objective

Keep rejected input from reaching the model, preserve the expected treatment and quality of legitimate inputs, and keep explanation output within the approved authorization and disclosure limits.

## Rationale

Serving protections depend on the model, its inputs, and the documented threat scenario. A control that changes input or rejects requests can also affect legitimate predictions and availability. Measure those effects against acceptance criteria for the adopted model. Explanations can expose internal information or sensitive features, so their use and disclosure require a separate decision.

## Scope

Applies to production Kubernetes inference endpoints with documented risks from manipulated inputs or explanation disclosure. Include the serving components, dependency-failure behavior, and alternate paths to the in-scope model or explanation output. Apply explanation requirements to endpoints that offer this output.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: The serving gateway or application component that enforces the adopted input policy before model execution, and the authorization and disclosure controls on each explanation path.
- Implementation Surface: Maintain the adopted input rules, dependency-failure decisions, evaluation criteria, and remediation thresholds. Verify expected dispositions at each in-scope path, including continued service for permitted input and defined behavior on failure. Evaluate the adopted protections using authorized representative inputs and record their measured effectiveness and effects on legitimate input quality and availability. Where explanations are offered, record authorized identities, permitted content, and any configured rate or volume bounds and verify actual behavior. Retain minimized decision and failure records and reassess after relevant model, integration, policy, or exposure changes.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; ML Engineering
