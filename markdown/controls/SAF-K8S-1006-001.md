# SAF-K8S-1006-001 - Regulatory compliance mapping for Kubernetes AI platforms

- Domain: D10 - Observability, Incident Response, and Governance
- Knowledge Area: 10.6 - Compliance and Governance
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Organizations must maintain a documented mapping from Kubernetes AI platform controls to applicable legal and regulatory obligations and to the security and assurance frameworks or criteria they have adopted. Record the applicable EU AI Act obligations by organizational role and system risk classification. Record NIST AI RMF outcomes for the adopted profile and AICPA Trust Services Criteria for the applicable SOC 2 engagement. Identify control coverage, supporting evidence, unresolved gaps and compensating controls.

## Objective

Support regulatory and assurance reviews with maintained, traceable relationships among applicable requirements, Kubernetes controls, observed evidence and unresolved gaps.

## Rationale

Legal obligations, voluntary frameworks and assurance criteria have different authority and scope. A maintained mapping gives reviewers a common record of applicable requirements, assessed evidence, overlapping coverage and work still needed.

## Scope

Applies to Kubernetes AI platforms in scope of legal obligations, adopted security or risk frameworks, or assurance engagements. Record the authority, applicable edition, system and engagement scope, jurisdiction, organizational role and risk classification as relevant. Review the mapping when an applicable obligation, adopted framework, system use or engagement scope changes.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: GRC platform compliance mapping module, regulatory change management process, and audit preparation workflow.
- Implementation Surface: Maintain controlled, reviewable records of source editions, scope and applicability decisions. Map applicable EU AI Act provisions using the relevant actor and risk classification. Track applicable NIST AI RMF Govern, Map, Measure and Manage outcomes for the adopted profile and Trust Services Criteria for the engagement scope. Include SSDF v1.1 and SP 800-218A task identifiers when applicable to the secure development scope. Link each decision to accountable owners, verified evidence and open gaps or approved compensating controls.
- Owner Primary: GRC/Compliance
- Owner Supporting: Security Architecture; Platform Engineering; Legal
