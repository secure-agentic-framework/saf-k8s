# SAF-K8S-0704-006 - Cloud provider contingency plans for managed Kubernetes services

- Domain: D07 - Storage, Multi-tenancy, and Resource Governance
- Knowledge Area: 7.4 - Cloud Provider Security Integration
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Maintain and exercise a customer-owned continuity and recovery plan for each governed managed Kubernetes service and its workloads and data. Identify provider-managed responsibilities, customer dependencies, approved recovery objectives and suitable recovery or migration paths.

## Objective

Demonstrate recovery of prioritized workloads and data within approved recovery time and recovery point objectives.

## Rationale

Provider control-plane commitments and customer workload recovery address different risks. Supported recovery capabilities and data portability depend on the service, tier, dependencies and protected data. Validate the chosen procedure by observing availability, integrity, recovery time and recovery point.

## Scope

Applies to governed managed Kubernetes services. Review the continuity plan at least annually and after material changes. Include workload and application state, relevant cloud dependencies, viable recovery destinations, and separately documented provider responsibilities and limitations.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Approved continuity plans, backup and restore exercises, tested recovery paths and retained corrective actions.
- Implementation Surface: Record provider and customer responsibilities, prerequisites and recovery objectives. Test approved export, restore and rebuilding paths for workload configuration, persistent application data and dependent resources. Use provider-supported control-plane procedures within their stated scope. Retain integrity, elapsed-time, recovery-point and corrective-action evidence from the actual exercises.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Cloud Platform Engineering; Platform Engineering; Legal; Procurement
