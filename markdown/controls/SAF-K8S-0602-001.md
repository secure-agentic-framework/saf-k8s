# SAF-K8S-0602-001 - Sigstore/cosign keyless signing and Rekor transparency logging

- Domain: D06 - Supply Chain, Images, and Admission Control
- Knowledge Area: 6.2 - Image Signing and Admission Enforcement
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Sign each governed production image digest with an approved Sigstore keyless identity. Obtain an OIDC token from an approved identity provider and present it to Fulcio for a short-lived signing certificate. Before deployment, verify the image signature, digest, certificate chain, signer identity, issuer and required Rekor inclusion and signing-time evidence.

## Objective

Establish digest-specific image integrity and signer identity using short-lived keyless certificates and verifiable transparency evidence. Assess build provenance separately.

## Rationale

Fulcio validates an identity token from a configured OIDC identity provider and binds its claims to an ephemeral public key in a short-lived certificate. Rekor makes signing events auditable through verifiable inclusion evidence. Neither identity nor log inclusion establishes the contents of a build process. Verify separately signed, digest-bound provenance and related policy where needed.

## Scope

Applies to all container images destined for production and staging clusters. Includes model-serving images, operator images, and sidecar images. Development-only images in isolated namespaces may be exempted with documented approval.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Post-build keyless signing, trusted verification and admission enforcement for the exact approved image digest.
- Implementation Surface: Pin signing and verification versions and configure approved OIDC issuers and identities, Fulcio and certificate-transparency trust, Rekor verification and trusted signing time. Retain verifiable bundles for the exact image digest. Verify directly at admission or require a protected exact-digest approval issued by a trusted verifier. Restrict approval writers and validate approval lifetime and invalidation on trust changes. Check all applicable admission paths and assess authenticated build provenance separately.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: DevSecOps / Release Engineering; Platform Engineering
