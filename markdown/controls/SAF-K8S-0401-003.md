# SAF-K8S-0401-003 - Aggregated ClusterRole governance

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.1 - Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Maturity: Foundational
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Organizations must review and restrict aggregated ClusterRole configurations to prevent controllers or operators from unintentionally granting elevated permissions through label-based role aggregation rules.

## Objective

Prevent unintended permission expansion caused by Kubernetes role aggregation, where the aggregation controller combines rules from matching ClusterRole objects into aggregate roles such as admin, edit, or view.

## Rationale

Kubernetes aggregated ClusterRoles use label selectors to automatically merge permissions from multiple ClusterRoles into a single effective role. An operator or chart can install or update a ClusterRole whose labels match an aggregate's selector, expanding that aggregate role's permissions. The aggregation controller selects ClusterRoles. Labels on a CustomResourceDefinition do not contribute RBAC rules. This creates a covert privilege escalation vector where installing a Helm chart or operator grants broader access than intended.

## Scope

Applies to all clusters where aggregated ClusterRoles are in use, including the default admin, edit, and view roles. Covers contributing ClusterRoles and aggregate selectors across cluster manifests, including those installed by operators, Helm charts, and custom-resource integrations. Particular attention is required during operator and CRD installation workflows.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Admission policies that validate aggregation labels on ClusterRole resources, and pre-deployment review of Helm charts and operator manifests for aggregation rule declarations.
- Implementation Surface: ClusterRole aggregationRule selectors and contributing ClusterRole labels, including the built-in aggregate-to-admin, aggregate-to-edit, and aggregate-to-view labels. Review changes to selectors, matched contributor labels and contributor permissions. Validate trusted contributors at admission and inspect the reconciled rules and effective bindings.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering
