# SAF-K8S-1004-001 - ML threat taxonomy per CTA-2114 mapped to Kubernetes

- Domain: D10 - Observability, Incident Response, and Governance
- Knowledge Area: 10.4 - AI and Supply Chain Threat Taxonomy
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Organizations must review the threat categories and boundaries in CTA-2114 (November 2025), Section 9, assess which apply to their Kubernetes-hosted ML systems, and map each applicable category to Kubernetes attack surfaces, control points, and detection mechanisms. The threat model must also address relevant system-specific threats beyond the Section 9 examples.

## Objective

Maintain a consistent threat classification and Kubernetes mapping for in-scope ML systems to support risk communication, detection development, and control planning.

## Rationale

Inconsistent threat classifications can leave gaps between threat modeling, detections, and control planning. CTA-2114 Section 9 provides a non-exhaustive list of ML threats. SAF-K8S requires teams to assess applicability and extend the threat model for the deployed system.

## Scope

Applies to ML-based systems operating on Kubernetes where CTA-2114 threat categories are relevant. Review the mapping when CTA-2114 is updated or when new workload types or trust boundaries change the threat model. Use the mapping in threat modeling, detection rule development, and incident classification.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Threat modeling documentation standards, detection rule taxonomy tagging, and incident classification schema.
- Implementation Surface: Review CTA-2114 Section 9, printed pages 16–17. Its entries are Data Poisoning, Model Poisoning, Label Modification, Targeted Poisoning Attacks, Poisoning Attacks, Model Disclosure, Data Disclosure, Evasion (Inference), Oracle Attack, and Denial of service due to inconsistent data. Treat extraction, inversion, and membership inference as forms discussed in the Oracle Attack entry. Record applicability, Kubernetes surfaces, control points, and detection mechanisms in the threat model. Include relevant threats outside the Section 9 list.
- Owner Primary: Security Architecture
- Owner Supporting: AI/ML Engineering; Detection Engineering; GRC/Compliance
