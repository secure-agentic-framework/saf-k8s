# SAF-K8S-0910-022 - Cross-cluster communication authorization policy enforcement

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.10 - Multi-Cluster and Federated AI Security
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Default-deny cross-cluster AI workload traffic and explicitly authorize the authenticated source identity, destination service or endpoint, and allowed operation where the service exposes separately governed operations.

## Objective

Allow a federated workload to reach only its approved peers, destinations and operations.

## Rationale

Authenticated encrypted transport can carry requests outside a peer's authorized scope. Bind authorization to the verified caller and evaluate it at a component that can distinguish the governed destination or operation.

## Scope

Applies to cross-cluster training, inference and pipeline traffic. Verify the network paths and the application operations governed by each policy, including actions attempted by an authenticated peer outside its scope.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Cross-cluster network enforcement and the receiving mesh, gateway or application policy for authenticated operation authorization.
- Implementation Surface: Apply compatible default-deny network rules for allowed peers and endpoints. Where operations are separately scoped, enforce exact caller and operation rules at a service mesh or application that can verify the caller and inspect that operation. Test the approved and disallowed operations with the same authenticated peer.
- Owner Primary: Network Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Security Engineering; ML Platform Engineering
