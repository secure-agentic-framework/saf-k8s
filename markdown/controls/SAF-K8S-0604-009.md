# SAF-K8S-0604-009 - AI workload vulnerability prioritization and remediation SLAs

- Domain: D06 - Supply Chain, Images, and Admission Control
- Knowledge Area: 6.4 - SBOMs and Vulnerability Intelligence
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Prioritize in-scope AI workload findings using evidenced workload context, severity, threat and exposure information. Define target deadlines, start times, responsible owners and escalation rules for applicable risk and workload classes. Route incomplete context for timely review. Verify remediation and govern exception approval, scope and expiry.

## Objective

Apply a reviewable prioritization and deadline policy to contextualized findings, recording ownership, verified resolution and missed targets.

## Rationale

Workload context, threat evidence, impact and uncertainty change remediation priority. Use an approved policy for the organization and keep severity inputs distinct from the final decision. Training, inference and other workload roles do not individually determine risk.

## Scope

Applies to all AI and ML workload images deployed in Kubernetes clusters, including inference servers, training jobs, data preprocessing pipelines, and model-serving infrastructure. Covers prioritization criteria, workload-tier risk ranking, and remediation SLA assignment. Does not cover exposure classification mechanics or automated rebuild pipeline execution, which are addressed by SAF-K8S-0604-008 and SAF-K8S-0604-010.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Vulnerability-management workflow assigns owners and deadlines, reevaluates open records, escalates missed or unresolved targets and verifies closure.
- Implementation Surface: Version the prioritization and deadline policy. Record the start event, due time, timezone and clock semantics, matched artifact and owner, exception scope and approval, expiry, escalation and verification of remediation. Preserve original targets and missed history, and reevaluate when context changes. Use approved production targets and test the workflow against incomplete evidence and expired exceptions.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; DevSecOps / Release Engineering
