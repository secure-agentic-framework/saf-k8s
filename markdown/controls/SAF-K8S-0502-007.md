# SAF-K8S-0502-007 - Kubernetes pod IP anti-spoofing enforcement and validation

- Domain: D05 - Network Security and Communication
- Knowledge Area: 5.2 - CNI Plugins and Pod Networking Security
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Identify all applicable Pod network paths and the effective source-address validation supported by the adopted CNI or provider. Enable and verify supported enforcement for managed Pod interfaces. Restrict documented exceptions to workloads with an authorized need for alternate source addresses. Record the approved treatment and residual risk for unsupported or unverified paths.

## Objective

Reduce source-address spoofing risk on covered Pod network paths and make topology limits and authorized exceptions explicit.

## Rationale

Source-address validation is enforced by the selected CNI or provider datapath. Coverage depends on implementation, version, address family, interface, chaining, and provider integration. Configuration and opt-out inventory establish the selected settings. Evaluate enforcement with approved evidence for each declared path. Some network functions require alternate addresses and need documented approval.

## Scope

Applies to governed Kubernetes Pod networking on managed and self-managed clusters. Identify managed endpoints, supported address families, chained and additional interfaces, host-network and unmanaged paths, provider constraints and approved exceptions. Record covered, unsupported and unverified paths and their approved treatment.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: The selected CNI or provider datapath's source-address validation for managed Pod interfaces, with separate controls governing exceptions and network-policy access.
- Implementation Surface: Record the applicable CNI or provider, version, IPAM and chaining mode, address families and supported datapath boundary. Verify effective enabled settings, managed endpoint coverage, and active exception settings. Assess host-network and additional interfaces, unmanaged and pre-existing Pods, and provider-specific paths. Retain approved evidence of enforcement for each declared path. Keep configuration, ordinary connectivity and enforcement evidence distinct. Record exception owners, compensating controls, residual gaps and drift review.
- Owner Primary: Network Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Security Operations
