# SAF-K8S-0908-005 - Model abuse logging and alerting

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.8 - Model Abuse and Extraction Prevention
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Collect scoped and privacy-minimized inference security telemetry, evaluate approved abuse indicators and verify alert delivery and review. Assess separately governed external disclosure monitoring where required by the threat model.

## Objective

Make suspicious serving behavior and applicable external disclosure events observable and actionable under the approved response process.

## Rationale

Telemetry coverage and alert routing are distinct from detection quality and response. Risk and use patterns determine useful indicators, normal comparators and escalation thresholds. External disclosure monitoring has a different collection and review boundary.

## Scope

Applies to governed inference traffic and deployed telemetry and alerting. Include actual identity and model attribution, policy coverage, approved indicators, quality measurements and response evidence. Verify any authorized external collection and correlation separately.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Actual serving telemetry, selected detection rules, protected alert delivery and the accountable review path.
- Implementation Surface: Exercise normal and selected benign control signals, bounded telemetry, correlation and delivery. Test actual thresholds, false-positive and missed-event criteria, owner response, and external monitoring only for approved sources and scope.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; Security Operations
