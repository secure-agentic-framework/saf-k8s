# SAF-K8S-0910-017 - Centralized audit logging for cross-cluster orchestration actions

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.10 - Multi-Cluster and Federated AI Security
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Collect orchestration audit records from each governed cluster in a separately operated store. Preserve authenticated source, actor, action, result and time. Test authorized retrieval, delivery gaps and retention when a source becomes unavailable.

## Objective

Keep enough independently retained evidence to reconstruct actual cross-cluster orchestration decisions.

## Rationale

Source-local records can become inaccessible. Central collection adds a distinct custody and review path, which depends on source registration, trustworthy transport, integrity and retention.

## Scope

Applies to governed cluster placement, routing and scheduling decisions and related central audit workflows. Establish effective event coverage and collector independence, time correlation and incident access in the actual deployment.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Audited orchestration events, independently operated collection and protected retention and review.
- Implementation Surface: Inventory sources and required events, validate source and actor attribution, protect forwarding and stored records, verify authorized and denied reads, and test loss and delayed delivery with actionable alerts.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; Security Engineering; SRE
