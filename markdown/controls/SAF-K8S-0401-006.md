# SAF-K8S-0401-006 - Organizational role separation for ML, platform, and security functions

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.1 - Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Maturity: Foundational
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Organizations must separate routine ML deployment and training, platform configuration and infrastructure, and security policy, audit, and approval duties through distinct functional Kubernetes roles and independently governed assignments. Because Kubernetes RBAC combines permissions from all applicable bindings, distinct role definitions alone do not ensure separation. Organizations must review effective permissions for each subject across identity-provider memberships and applicable RoleBindings and ClusterRoleBindings, including AI operator resources, and prevent or detect conflicting functional grants. Cross-functional assignments require independent approval; exceptions require documented risk acceptance.

## Objective

Prevent any subject from holding conflicting model deployment, infrastructure change, and security oversight permissions without independent review, reducing the risk of unauthorized or unreviewed changes to AI infrastructure and operations.

## Rationale

Kubernetes RBAC grants are additive across applicable bindings, so separate ML, platform, and security Role definitions do not prevent one identity from accumulating their combined permissions through multiple bindings or group memberships. Effective-identity review and independent approval are needed to preserve separation while allowing routine, separately scoped functional access. Exceptions remain documented and governed as accepted risk.

## Scope

Applies to all clusters running AI and ML workloads. Covers core Kubernetes resources, AI operator CRDs, identity-provider group membership and nesting, and effective access through namespace RoleBindings and cluster-wide ClusterRoleBindings. Review assignments before approval and periodically; exceptions require documented risk acceptance and review.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Independently governed identity and group assignments plus review of each subject's effective Kubernetes permissions across applicable RoleBindings and ClusterRoleBindings; distinct Roles and ClusterRoles provide scoped routine grants but do not by themselves prevent additive cross-functional access.
- Implementation Surface: Maintain separately owned, least-privilege Roles and ClusterRoles for ML, platform, and security functions; map approved identity-provider groups through independently reviewed bindings; evaluate effective subject permissions across group memberships, RoleBindings, ClusterRoleBindings, and AI operator resources before cross-functional changes and periodically; block detected conflicts unless an exception has documented risk acceptance and independent approval; and record its risk owner, rationale, compensating controls, expiry, and re-review date.
- Owner Primary: Identity and Access Management
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; ML Engineering; Security Engineering
