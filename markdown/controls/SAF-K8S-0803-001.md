# SAF-K8S-0803-001 - NVLink and NVSwitch traffic isolation for multi-GPU training

- Domain: D08 - GPU, Accelerator, and Confidential Computing
- Knowledge Area: 8.3 - High-Performance Interconnect Security
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Verify the accelerator interconnect isolation required by the tenant threat model on the exact supported GPU, switch, driver and virtualization configuration. Restrict allocation and topology visibility to approved scopes and use independently verified separation where the required shared-fabric boundary is unavailable.

## Objective

Keep cross-trust workloads within verified accelerator placement and interconnect boundaries.

## Rationale

Physical accelerator topology, fabric partitioning and workload placement are distinct. Kubernetes placement and IP network policy govern their own layers. Application library configuration affects transport selection and does not establish hardware peer isolation.

## Scope

Applies to governed multi-GPU platforms using NVLink, NVSwitch or comparable interconnects. Record actual switch and GPU generation, firmware, software, sharing model, provider assurance, workload placement and authorized topology disclosure.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Vendor-supported physical fabric or dedicated allocation boundary, with separately verified placement and disclosure policy.
- Implementation Surface: Inventory actual devices, interconnect and mode. Obtain applicable vendor or provider evidence for the exact isolation properties. Independently verify supported orchestration declarations, actual allocations and placement, and topology disclosure. Keep unknown shared-fabric configurations outside cross-trust use.
- Owner Primary: GPU Infrastructure Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; Network Engineering; Security Engineering
