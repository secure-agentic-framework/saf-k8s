# SAF-K8S-0905-049 - OCI model registry tag immutability and overwrite prevention

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.5 - AI Supply Chain and Model Lifecycle
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

OCI registries that store or distribute approved models must prevent protected version tags from being overwritten or reused. Verify the effective protection for each governed identity and operation, including elevated roles where the selected registry permits exceptions.

## Objective

Ensure that OCI registries storing approved models prevent post-review tag mutation or overwrite that could redirect downstream consumers to a different artifact.

## Rationale

Mutable release tags can redirect a consumer to a different artifact. Registry products can apply tag protection through different settings and authorization rules. An effective global or privileged update grant can defeat repository-scoped protection where the product permits it. Digest-pinned consumers protect content identity independently.

## Scope

Applies to OCI registries and model registries backed by OCI artifacts that store approved models. Covers immutable-tag registry settings, blocked overwrite attempts, and audit visibility into tag mutation enforcement.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: OCI registry configuration and audit controls for model artifact tags.
- Implementation Surface: Use the selected registry’s supported controls for protected repositories and tag patterns. Observe the initial publish, overwrite and deletion refusal, unchanged digest, an approved distinct tag and attributable logs. Review elevated principals, overrides, retention and garbage collection, and verify equivalent behavior for the deployed version.
- Owner Primary: ML Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; DevOps Engineering
