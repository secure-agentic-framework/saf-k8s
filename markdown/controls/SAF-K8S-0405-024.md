# SAF-K8S-0405-024 - Break-glass credential automatic expiration and revocation enforcement

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.5 - Identity Abuse Detection and Mitigation
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Automatically end governed emergency access at the approved deadline. Enforce expiry or invalidation at the actual credential authority where supported, withdraw temporary authorization, and verify that the previously allowed operation becomes unavailable after the window.

## Objective

Retire effective emergency access within the approved deadline while preserving independent authorized operations.

## Rationale

A deadline annotation or kubeconfig file does not itself retire the underlying credential. Authenticator expiry, issuer invalidation and authorization withdrawal are different mechanisms. A still-authenticated caller can be denied by withdrawal of its temporary grant.

## Scope

Applies to inventoried emergency access paths. Identify the actual token, certificate, provider session or authority, its validity and invalidation behavior, the relying authorization boundary, and how continued or cached access is handled.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Effective credential lifecycle and an automatically reconciled least-privilege authorization deadline.
- Implementation Surface: Observe authorized operation before the deadline, actual credential expiry or scoped grant withdrawal where applicable, reliable deadline reconciliation, rejection of former access, independent access preservation and protected evidence. Apply mechanism-specific limits when immediate revocation is unavailable.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Identity and Access Management
