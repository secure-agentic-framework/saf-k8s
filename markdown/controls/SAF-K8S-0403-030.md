# SAF-K8S-0403-030 - Environment variable secret injection exception governance

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.3 - Secrets Management
- Maturity: Foundational
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Record each approved production deviation from the Secret-backed environment restriction in an authoritative exception registry. Require a bounded workload scope, owner, approver, rationale, compensating controls and mandatory review or expiry, and verify what happens when an exception becomes overdue.

## Objective

Allow only justified, attributable and timely exceptions and require verified action when a deviation loses approval.

## Rationale

Secret-backed environment delivery can expose values through process output and diagnostics. An exception record must be bound to its governed scope and remain current so evidence supports each deviation.

## Scope

Applies to deviations covered by SAF-K8S-0403-029. Identify applicable workload and injection paths, approval roles and processes for existing workloads when exceptions expire.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Authoritative exception approvals, deadline reconciliation, and evidence connecting the registry and actual admission or remediation.
- Implementation Surface: Reconcile detected uses with the exception registry. Record scope, compensating settings, reviewer and due date. Verify current, incomplete, unlisted and overdue exceptions at the governed boundary, and document remediation of existing deviations.
- Owner Primary: Application Development
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; Platform Engineering
