# SAF-K8S-0804-006 - Sealed storage binding of encrypted AI artifacts to enclave measurements

- Domain: D08 - GPU, Accelerator, and Confidential Computing
- Knowledge Area: 8.4 - Confidential Computing for AI Workloads
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Where confidential workloads require artifact use to depend on approved platform state, configure supported platform sealing or a trusted verifier and release authority that conditions artifact-key access on the approved claims. Document which identity and state the policy covers and govern permitted updates and recovery.

## Objective

Demonstrate approved artifact access and rejection of policy-relevant identity or state changes at the selected hardware or external release boundary.

## Rationale

Platform sealing can restrict key use to a measured or signed identity defined by the implementation. Remote appraisal and conditional release use a separate trust boundary. Authenticated encryption protects the ciphertext and declared context, while hardware identity and key custody need independent evidence.

## Scope

Applies to protected model and data artifacts with a stated confidential state-binding requirement. Record authorized platform profiles and measurements, identity and policy, artifact-key custody, freshness for remote appraisal, update and recovery paths, and trustworthy evidence sources.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: The supported platform unsealing policy or the verified external artifact-key release decision, with authenticated encryption bound to the intended artifact and context.
- Implementation Surface: Identify the policy and supported identity granularity. Exercise the approved identity, policy-relevant mismatch, artifact integrity checks and authorized update or recovery. Retain separate cryptographic, enforcement and platform evidence.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Key Management; ML Platform Engineering; GPU Infrastructure Engineering
