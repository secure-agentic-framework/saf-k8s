# SAF-K8S-0104-007 - Emergency Kubernetes patch deployment procedures

- Domain: D01 - Control Plane and Cluster Hardening
- Knowledge Area: 1.4 - CIS Benchmarks and Patch Management
- Maturity: Foundational
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Maintain and exercise an emergency change process for applicable Kubernetes and platform patches. Define severity and exposure triggers, accountable approval, provider and component responsibilities, supported execution sequence, validation, recovery decision points and communication.

## Objective

Deploy urgent patches within established risk objectives while preserving service availability and accountable recovery decisions.

## Rationale

Emergency change urgency comes from organizational risk and supported vendor or upstream guidance. Provider ownership, Kubernetes version skew, dependencies and reversibility constrain execution. Reusing a normal change plan requires explicit emergency decision ownership and evidence.

## Scope

Applies to governed Kubernetes control plane, nodes, operating system, runtime and add-on patches. Identify responsibilities, urgency, applicable provider support, backward compatibility, rollout and recovery procedures for each covered boundary.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Evaluated emergency change process, supported technical execution and exercised service and security acceptance checkpoints.
- Implementation Surface: Validate alert and approval paths, current releases, accepted version skew, workload and add-on checks, backup and restoration responsibilities, communications and reported outcomes. Exercise applicable limited rollout, continuation, abort and recovery actions and link supporting records.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Change Management; Incident Response
