# SAF-K8S-0405-019 - Break-glass recovery procedure definition

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.5 - Identity Abuse Detection and Mitigation
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Maintain an approved, current emergency access procedure for each governed Kubernetes access path. Document which normal dependency can fail, the independent approved fallback, the exact recovery operation, the authorized roles and how temporary access is withdrawn.

## Objective

Give authorized responders an actionable and reviewable recovery procedure for the specified authentication failure.

## Rationale

A written procedure identifies the accountable choices and prerequisites. Real recovery depends on the selected failure mode, continuing authentication and authorization, current operational approvals and verified restoration.

## Scope

Applies to the governed primary and emergency access paths, including external identity and managed control-plane integrations. Cover controlled credential storage and activation, evidence and restoration. Validate actual execution under SAF-K8S-0405-020.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Authoritative governed runbook and approved activation and disengagement requirements.
- Implementation Surface: Define approved owners, trigger, independent path, permitted operation, approval evidence, failure stop conditions, withdrawal and exercise cadence. Verify the maintained procedure meets those requirements and retain results and site exceptions.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Identity and Access Management
