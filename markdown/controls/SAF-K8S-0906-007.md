# SAF-K8S-0906-007 - Integrity verification at each training data transformation stage

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.6 - Training Data Integrity and Poisoning Defense
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Verify exact inputs and outputs at governed training transformation boundaries. Bind them to trusted stage identities and expectations, and prevent unverified or inconsistent artifacts from reaching downstream consumers.

## Objective

Reject missing, altered or inconsistent stage artifacts before they propagate to training.

## Rationale

A successful stage and valid schema alone leave output integrity unknown. Verification can bind exact bytes to a trusted stage expectation and gate downstream use.

## Scope

Applies to governed transformations through the input consumed by training. Pin expected hashes where outputs are deterministic and independently approved. Verify provenance and configured invariants for variable outputs.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Stage input and output verification with downstream release or consumption gates.
- Implementation Surface: Record immutable stage artifacts and producer identities, verify digests and applicable invariants, reject incorrect or mislabeled outputs and verify the downstream consumer uses the approved artifact.
- Owner Primary: Data Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; Security Engineering
