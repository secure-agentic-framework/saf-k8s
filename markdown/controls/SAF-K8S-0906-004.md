# SAF-K8S-0906-004 - Statistical drift, outlier, and input validation for training data poisoning detection

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.6 - Training Data Integrity and Poisoning Defense
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Before training, verify approved dataset schema, input bounds and selected statistical quality indicators against a representative versioned baseline. Hold invalid data for review and document the limits of those checks.

## Objective

Keep invalid or anomalous data out of the governed training pipeline until reviewed.

## Rationale

Schema, drift and outlier checks detect only defined discrepancies. Establish data provenance, access and integrity alongside statistical screening, and use independent evidence to investigate causes.

## Scope

Applies to governed training intake and feature refresh. Verify deployed datasets, sampling and shifts, configured thresholds, action on invalid data, and relevant integrity controls.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Pre-training quality policy and the durable release gate.
- Implementation Surface: Record the eligible baseline and sampled inputs, schema and bounds, comparison outputs, authorized investigation and release decisions. Evaluate representative deployment coverage, sensitivity and operational limits.
- Owner Primary: Data Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; ML Engineering
