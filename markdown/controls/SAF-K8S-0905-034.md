# SAF-K8S-0905-034 - Canary and A/B candidate version isolation and traffic-splitting integrity

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.5 - AI Supply Chain and Model Lifecycle
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Separate candidate and stable serving versions at the applicable traffic and workload boundaries. Restrict who can change the approved traffic split, verify the effective routing and independent candidate-to-stable isolation, and confirm stable availability during candidate withdrawal.

## Objective

Ensure multi-version serving infrastructure keeps candidate and stable versions isolated and enforces approved traffic distribution during staged rollout.

## Rationale

Access controls on routing configuration limit unauthorized changes. Effective network policy can protect approved version-specific paths. Shared resources and device topology require independent controls where they affect the approved rollout design.

## Scope

Applies to all multi-version model serving on Kubernetes, including canary deployments, A/B testing, and blue-green deployments. Covers resource and runtime isolation between versions and integrity of traffic-splitting configuration. Excludes rollback triggers and recovery automation, which are addressed separately.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Canary or A/B rollout controller configuration and service-mesh or ingress routing policy.
- Implementation Surface: Verify actual route and rollout-controller configuration, latest applied traffic weights or cohort rules, authority for changes and active version-specific policy. Observe permitted and denied traffic from the documented paths. Verify resource, placement and accelerator isolation where required.
- Owner Primary: ML Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: DevOps Engineering; Security Engineering
