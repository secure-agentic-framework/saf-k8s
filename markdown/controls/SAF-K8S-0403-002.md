# SAF-K8S-0403-002 - External secrets management integration

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.3 - Secrets Management
- Maturity: Foundational
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Use an approved external secret lifecycle and delivery integration for governed workloads where policy requires it. Verify access to the actual source, the selected direct or synchronized delivery path, consumer adoption, and handling of unavailable or stale source data.

## Objective

Make the actual secret source, scoped actor, delivery, freshness and failure behavior verifiable.

## Rationale

External storage, synchronization, direct mounting, authority-side rotation and application reload are distinct. An operator can create a Kubernetes Secret, and delivery freshness depends on the actual refresh mode, platform and consumer. Storage and audit protections require verified settings.

## Scope

Applies to governed workload secret integrations, selected providers and versions, direct or synchronized copies, consuming workloads, and documented bootstrap or lifecycle exceptions.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: The approved provider, authorized controller or driver, secret delivery and workload consumption, and an independently tested recovery process.
- Implementation Surface: Verify provider and operator identity, trust, version and refresh settings. Where an operator writes Kubernetes Secrets, permit only approved actors and protect the resulting copies. Exercise source unavailability, stale behavior, recovery and application consumption. Verify upstream credential rotation and revocation separately when required.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Application Development
