# SAF-K8S-0908-010 - Inference privacy budget tracking and threshold enforcement

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.8 - Model Abuse and Extraction Prevention
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Before serving a fresh differentially private release, enforce an approved cumulative privacy budget for its privacy unit, neighboring relation and composition scope. Coordinate spend across all relevant release paths that share that scope.

## Objective

Keep cumulative privacy loss within the reviewed bound for each governed release history.

## Rationale

Fresh releases that access protected data can compose across callers and models. Post-processing an existing differentially private artifact without additional access to protected data does not incur fresh privacy loss for that artifact's guarantee.

## Scope

Applies to Kubernetes-hosted serving paths that execute new differentially private mechanisms. Define all overlapping protected populations and releases in the accounting scope. Assess pure post-processing and non-private releases separately.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: The data-dependent release boundary and its authoritative coordinated privacy accountant.
- Implementation Surface: Bind scope and mechanism to a reviewed accounting contract, including compatible privacy parameters, composition, overlap assumptions and thresholds. Atomically reserve or charge before release, retain durable state across restarts, and deny releases when accounting state is unavailable or the approved budget would be exceeded. Test allowed and denied releases across applicable caller, model and route boundaries.

- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; Privacy Engineering
