# SAF-K8S-0906-006 - Training data provenance tracking from ingestion through model training

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.6 - Training Data Integrity and Poisoning Defense
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Record authenticated and durable training lineage from approved source through transformation to the produced model. Verify the input, receipt and artifact identities and exact digests before release. Support backward and forward tracing for affected data versions.

## Objective

Trace released models to their approved inputs and identify outputs affected by a source version or transformation.

## Rationale

A successful training job can omit what it consumed. A release gate can require evidence binding source, transformations and the training run to the exact model artifact.

## Scope

Applies to governed training pipelines, metadata stores, receipt producers and model release paths. Verify access, retention, queryability and recall against actual workflows.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Trusted lineage records at stage boundaries and a policy decision on the release path.
- Implementation Surface: Bind source, run and artifact identifiers and digests to authenticated producer receipts. Reject incomplete or inconsistent lineage and test trace queries in both directions.
- Owner Primary: Data Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; Security Engineering
