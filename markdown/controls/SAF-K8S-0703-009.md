# SAF-K8S-0703-009 - GPU spending limits and budget enforcement

- Domain: D07 - Storage, Multi-tenancy, and Resource Governance
- Knowledge Area: 7.3 - Resource Governance and Priority
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Establish an approved GPU spending budget for each applicable team or namespace. Use attributable metering, timely alerts and a reviewed workload response where the platform supports it.

## Objective

Limit the risk and duration of GPU spending overruns and verify that authorized cost responses are scoped, timely and effective.

## Rationale

Kubernetes quotas can bound declared requests and provider notifications can report estimated spending. Billing data and notification delays can permit further consumption before action. Financial spend limits and workload control require separate evidence.

## Scope

Applies to managed or self-operated GPU environments covered by the budget policy. Document accountable teams, currency, window, usage and billing sources, confidence and lag, approved notifications and workload response, exceptions and continuity needs.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Verified capacity or rate policy for declared requests, attributable actual cost monitoring, and an approved provider integration or scoped Kubernetes workload controller for actions.
- Implementation Surface: Reconcile workload and device usage to actual or estimated charges by budget scope and period. Verify alert delivery, delayed or unavailable input handling and the approved response. Where authorized, exercise scoped suspension or scale-down and preserve unaffected workloads. Verify the exact scope and limitations of any supported provider spend cap.
- Owner Primary: FinOps
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; ML Engineering
