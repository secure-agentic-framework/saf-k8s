# SAF-K8S-0405-004 - Security awareness for Kubernetes and GPU administrators

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.5 - Identity Abuse Detection and Mitigation
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Train designated Kubernetes and GPU administrators in role-relevant security practices and recognition and reporting of social engineering. Record approved curricula, targeted roles, verified completion and renewal requirements. Apply any approved training-dependent access workflow and verify its operation.

## Objective

Establish documented role coverage and verified training activity and assessment for personnel with designated platform responsibilities.

## Rationale

Role-specific exercises can cover relevant operating procedures and risk scenarios. Training completion records, verified learning, automated provisioning and actual incident behavior provide distinct evidence.

## Scope

Applies to designated personnel with defined Kubernetes, cloud, GPU or production delivery responsibilities. Identify affected access categories, approved training topics, assignment triggers and renewal deadlines.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Approved role-specific curriculum and reviewer records, measured assessment and effective linkage to provisioning or review policies where required.
- Implementation Surface: Review role inventory, curricula and practical exercises, assessment and verified completion, deadlines, exceptions and record handling. Where governed, verify that current, pending and overdue records change access review or provisioning appropriately.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Identity and Access Management; People Operations
