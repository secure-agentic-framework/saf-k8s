# SAF-K8S-0910-004 - Federated learning cross-cluster coordination security

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.10 - Multi-Cluster and Federated AI Security
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Federated learning spanning Kubernetes clusters must define participant and trust boundaries and use reviewed, compatible controls for participant authentication and the confidentiality and integrity of transmitted information. Where the approved threat model calls for privacy protections or resilience to malicious participants, select and validate mechanisms whose interfaces and assumptions compose for the deployed protocol. Document residual risks and the evidence required for the federation.

## Objective

Coordinate approved federated-learning rounds across clusters with attributable participants, protected communication paths, and protocol-specific privacy and integrity controls appropriate to the documented data, participant, and operational threat assumptions.

## Rationale

Cross-cluster deployments add trust, enrollment, transport, protocol, and operational boundaries. Verify service identity and participant-to-cluster binding where the federation relies on them. Assess protection in transit, during aggregation, and across repeated releases independently. Secure aggregation and some robust aggregation methods can require different inputs or interfaces. Verify the selected mechanisms together against the documented assumptions.

## Scope

Applies to federated learning deployments spanning Kubernetes clusters, including cross-silo, cross-cloud, and separately administered participant or aggregation clusters. The required mechanisms depend on the implemented protocol, information sensitivity, applicable privacy requirements, participant threat model, and operational boundary. SAF-K8S-0901-005 addresses participant isolation and complementary federated learning requirements.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: The deployed federation enrollment and coordination path, including its service-identity and transport configuration, round admission and membership policy, and the aggregation and privacy mechanisms selected for the documented threat model.
- Implementation Surface: Document participants, trust domains, credential issuers and enrollment, round protocol, protected data paths, and assumptions about participant behavior and availability. Verify service authentication and transmission protection at each applicable boundary. Where required, document the selected secure aggregation, differential privacy, or Byzantine resilience mechanisms and demonstrate their compatibility and behavior in the adopted protocol. Retain applicable privacy accounting, release policy, and residual risks. Define and verify monitoring and audit coverage where required by the system policy.
- Owner Primary: ML Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; Privacy Engineering; Platform Engineering
