# SAF-K8S-0703-005 - AI workload resource exhaustion guardrails

- Domain: D07 - Storage, Multi-tenancy, and Resource Governance
- Knowledge Area: 7.3 - Resource Governance and Priority
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Enforce workload-specific resource and active-runtime bounds, controlled job admission and queue backpressure, and approved training data ingress limits. Where needed, limit aggregate declared requests by namespace. Verify each boundary and the defined recovery objective in the applicable system.

## Objective

Bound declared resource requests and active job execution, regulate queued work and ingest, and recover within the approved service objectives.

## Rationale

Unbounded queued jobs, resource requests, oversized inputs and jobs without deadlines can consume shared capacity. Kubernetes can limit declared resources and requested storage. The queue and data-ingest components govern their own admission, backpressure and byte limits. Verify each component's behavior and the combined service recovery.

## Scope

Applies to Kubernetes clusters hosting AI workloads. Cover per-job resource and active-lifetime limits, applicable namespace quotas, actual job or application queue admission and backpressure, and approved byte or object limits at training data ingest. Include documented service and recovery objectives.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Kubernetes admission and Job controller, the adopted workload admission or application queue controller, and the actual training data ingest component.
- Implementation Surface: Use Job activeDeadlineSeconds for active execution bounds, LimitRange for per-container defaults and ceilings, and ResourceQuota for aggregate declared-resource or requested-storage limits. Apply byte and object limits at the actual ingest path before persistence or queue admission. Verify scheduler Workload status where applicable, actual queue backpressure, allowed and rejected inputs, and measured recovery.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: SRE; AI/ML Engineering
