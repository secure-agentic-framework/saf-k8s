# SAF-K8S-0603-009 - In-toto and SLSA provenance attestation generation

- Domain: D06 - Supply Chain, Images, and Admission Control
- Knowledge Area: 6.3 - Attestation, Provenance, and Cryptographic Assurance
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Require trusted build workflows to generate in-toto SLSA provenance for the exact OCI image manifest or index digest being promoted. Record required build inputs, builder identity and invocation metadata. Publish a signed, digest-bound attestation and verify that it can be retrieved by the intended consumer.

## Objective

Verify which approved builder attested to the exact promoted image digest and evaluate the reported inputs and build parameters against policy.

## Rationale

A verified image signature authenticates a signing identity. A verified provenance statement supplies authenticated build claims for an exact subject digest. Confidence in those claims depends on builder isolation, the required fields, and the consumer's trust policy. Assess any claimed SLSA level separately.

## Scope

Applies to all container images in production and staging registries, including model-serving images, training images, and infrastructure operator images. Third-party vendor images must have equivalent provenance attestations verified or compensating controls documented.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: CI/CD pipeline provenance generation and registry attachment step.
- Implementation Surface: Record the applicable in-toto Statement and SLSA provenance versions, attested manifest or index digest, allowed builder and signing identities, invocation and expected inputs, and the supported OCI discovery method. Verify digest binding, signature and predicate after publication and after relevant promotion or replication.
- Owner Primary: DevSecOps / Release Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; Platform Engineering
