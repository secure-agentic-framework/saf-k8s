# SAF-K8S-0801-014 - MPS and time-slicing side-channel risk assessment

- Domain: D08 - GPU, Accelerator, and Confidential Computing
- Knowledge Area: 8.1 - GPU Device Plugins and Resource Allocation
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Assess shared-accelerator side-channel risks for the exact deployed mode and approved workload trust boundary, using applicable vendor documentation, public advisories and configuration evidence. Restrict or quarantine unsupported mixed-trust configurations and record review, mitigations and exceptions.

## Objective

Use configuration-specific evidence to decide which workloads may share accelerator resources and enforce that decision.

## Rationale

Different sharing modes have different documented hardware, visibility and scheduling guarantees. Memory sanitization addresses a separate property from ongoing shared-resource exposure. Reliance on a shared mode requires a documented threat and residual-risk decision.

## Scope

Applies to MPS, time-sliced and equivalent governed pools accepting sensitive or mutually untrusted workloads. Include exact platform versions and mode, tenant classifications, approved control paths and review triggers.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Threat and configuration review and verified admission and allocation eligibility by workload risk class.
- Implementation Surface: Maintain a reviewed configuration and risk record using applicable primary evidence and advisory disposition. Enforce verified workload and pool eligibility, reserve a reviewed alternative as required, and track configuration changes, exceptions and reassessment. Unavailable evidence keeps mixed-trust approval unresolved.
- Owner Primary: ML Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; Data Protection Office
