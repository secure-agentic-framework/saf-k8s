# SAF-K8S-0403-018 - AI platform cryptographic key access domain separation

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.3 - Secrets Management
- Maturity: Foundational
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Enforce a key and operation policy for governed training, serving, signing and governance actors. Verify each trusted workload or operator identity, its effective authorization at the key authority, and the approved separation of crypto use from policy administration.

## Objective

Prevent unapproved cryptographic operations and preserve attributable evidence for each supported key and identity domain.

## Rationale

Kubernetes workload identity and its mapping to the key authority are distinct from KMS permissions. Audit supports attribution and review. The deciding authorization rule and effective key operation establish prevention.

## Scope

Applies to governed application cryptographic keys, identities, provider bindings and authorized administrators. Distinguish each operation and key and any separately managed Kubernetes API-storage key.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Provider key and operation authorization, directly verified workload identity binding, and separate policy administration.
- Implementation Surface: Verify the actual principal and workload identity binding, exact key and operation grants, HSM or provider semantics and alternate credentials. Exercise in-domain and unrelated operations, policy changes and removal, and correlate actual provider and relevant Kubernetes audit evidence.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; ML Engineering; Key Management/Cryptography
