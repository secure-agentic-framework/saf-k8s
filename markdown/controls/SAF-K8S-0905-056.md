# SAF-K8S-0905-056 - External model publisher identity and provenance metadata verification

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.5 - AI Supply Chain and Model Lifecycle
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Organizations must verify available provenance metadata for externally sourced models, including publisher identity and available commit, release, or signature provenance, before the models are approved for internal use.

## Objective

Preserve chain of custody for external model intake by validating publisher identity and available provenance metadata before internal acceptance.

## Rationale

Even when a model passes malware or behavioral screening, deployment remains unsafe if the organization cannot establish who published it and what provenance metadata accompanies the artifact. Provenance verification provides the chain-of-custody context needed before internal use.

## Scope

Applies to all externally sourced models admitted for internal use. Covers publisher identity verification, model card or source metadata review, and commit-signature or release-signature provenance checks. Excludes broader trust-signal risk assessment and disposition review.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Provenance verification workflow before internal acceptance.
- Implementation Surface: Publisher identity checks, model card or source metadata review, commit-signature or release-signature validation, and documented provenance verification records for approved external models.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; ML Engineering

- Strength Reason Code: partial-control-coverage
- Applicability Condition: Only for organization-defined externally sourced model artifacts whose intake process requires signed release provenance and whose supplier trust root is independently established.

#### Framework Mapping Notes

NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 SR-4 requires documenting, monitoring, and maintaining valid provenance for organization-defined systems, components, and associated data. This control contributes an intake check: a restricted verifier accepts a fixture release only when its signature, publisher/source/release/commit metadata, model bytes, and model-card bytes match expected values; paired disposable-cluster receipts include unsigned, untrusted-key, mismatched-publisher, and changed-content comparisons. The fixture's key and publisher are synthetic and do not establish a real supplier identity. It does not demonstrate provenance maintenance, change monitoring, provenance transfer/custody, or complete organization intake coverage.

### NIST-800-53R5 SR-4(1)

- Framework: NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0
- Requirement Title: Identity
- Relation Type: supports
- Strength: moderate
- Applicability: conditional
- Strength Reason Code: partial-control-coverage
- Applicability Condition: Only where the organization independently binds the trusted key or certificate identity to an approved model supplier and defines the covered supply-chain elements and processes.

#### Framework Mapping Notes

NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 SR-4(1) requires establishing and maintaining unique identification of organization-defined supply-chain elements, processes, and personnel. This control checks that a signature validates under a separately supplied fixture public key and that signed publisher/source/commit metadata matches the expected release. The local publisher name and signing keys are generated by the fixture, so this does not independently identify a real supplier or establish an organization's maintained supplier inventory. Adoption must bind key fingerprints or certificate identities to approved supplier records, define rotation/revocation and source-identifier rules, and retain the release decision.
