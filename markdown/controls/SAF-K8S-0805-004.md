# SAF-K8S-0805-004 - Accelerator sharing risk and eligible workload placement

- Domain: D08 - GPU, Accelerator, and Confidential Computing
- Knowledge Area: 8.5 - GPU Workload Auditing and Monitoring
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Assess residual information disclosure risk for each governed accelerator sharing profile and restrict sensitive workloads to approved placement based on trust domain and documented isolation evidence. Require an attributable, expiring approval for any allowable exception.

## Objective

Reduce cross-trust information disclosure risk through evidence-backed accelerator configuration, placement constraints and recurring review.

## Rationale

Accelerator modes differ in resource sharing and documented isolation. Kubernetes admission and placement controls establish eligibility and scheduling behavior. Residual hardware risk depends on the actual device, firmware, driver, sharing mode and other shared paths.

## Scope

Applies to governed environments sharing accelerators or supporting infrastructure across workloads or trust domains. Record sensitivity, trust boundaries, actual vendor, model and profile, approved eligibility, unknown risks and exception ownership.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Versioned accelerator placement policy, protected node inventory and assignment, approved configuration evidence and governed exceptions.
- Implementation Surface: Maintain an approved profile and eligibility matrix. Exercise rejected unknown or disallowed placement, eligible placement, and applicable exception expiration. Verify actual inventory, workload placement and shared-resource assumptions with applicable vendor and deployment evidence. Retain the residual-risk decision and its review triggers.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; GPU Infrastructure Engineering; Risk Management
