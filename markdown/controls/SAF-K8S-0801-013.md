# SAF-K8S-0801-013 - Evidence for cross-trust eligibility of shared accelerator allocations

- Domain: D08 - GPU, Accelerator, and Confidential Computing
- Knowledge Area: 8.1 - GPU Device Plugins and Resource Allocation
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Authorize cross-trust use of shared accelerator allocations only when approved, configuration-specific evidence supports the required memory confidentiality boundary. Otherwise prohibit cross-trust use and assign a verified alternative or keep the allocation unavailable.

## Objective

Prevent exposure from unverified shared allocation reuse by enforcing an evidence-backed trust and eligibility decision.

## Rationale

The sharing mode, hardware memory isolation and sequential sanitization are distinct properties. A device allocation, workload exit, reset or sharing configuration alone cannot establish those security properties. Verify the actual product, mode, version and transition.

## Scope

Applies to governed MPS, time-sliced and equivalent sharing configurations used across trust domains or sensitivity levels, including successive allocations and approved alternatives.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Configuration-specific confidentiality evidence and effective allocation eligibility, quarantine or verified separation.
- Implementation Surface: Inventory hardware, firmware, software and effective mode. Record applicable vendor or provider properties and independently reviewed evidence, scope the eligibility decision to the relevant allocation and workloads, and verify the deployed policy and handling of missing or stale evidence.
- Owner Primary: ML Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; Data Protection Office
