# SAF-K8S-0404-001 - cert-manager deployment and Issuer configuration

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.4 - Certificate Management
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Operate a supported certificate lifecycle system using approved issuer and certificate policies. Verify issuance through the selected CA and the resulting identity, certificate properties, key handling, renewals and service reload. Assess revocation at the actual CA and relying parties.

## Objective

Provision and renew trusted certificates within defined operating and custody requirements, and separately verify retirement and revocation where required.

## Rationale

cert-manager automates certificate issuance, renewal scheduling and configured key rotation. The issuing authority, trust-store distribution, service reload, effective revocation and key custody require independently verified controls.

## Scope

Applies to governed Kubernetes certificate consumers and issuer integrations, including equivalent managed services. Record issuer scope, CA type and usage, and deployment-specific restrictions, trust anchors and authority responsibilities.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Approved issuer and Certificate configuration, observed controller decisions, X.509 verification, and monitored renewal and deployment outcomes.
- Implementation Surface: Verify the controller, namespace or cluster issuer scope, actual chain and intended endpoint identity, key type and custody, validity and rollover windows, consumer reload, and separate provider revocation where required. Record provider and client platform gaps.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering
