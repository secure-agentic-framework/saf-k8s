# SAF-K8S-0703-018 - GPU cost attribution metering and billing correlation

- Domain: D07 - Storage, Multi-tenancy, and Resource Governance
- Knowledge Area: 7.3 - Resource Governance and Priority
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Record GPU requests and assigned capacity, observed device telemetry, and provider or internal cost records separately. Use stable workload identities and validity intervals to attribute each applicable measure to namespace, team and run. Reconcile charge records and disclose shared, estimated and unallocated amounts.

## Objective

Produce auditable workload-level attribution with a traceable distinction between capacity, measured activity and source charges.

## Rationale

Resource requests and allocations represent capacity. Device exporters measure specific hardware signals. Provider charges use provider meters and financial rules. Reliable attribution joins these different records through validated identities, times and allocation policy.

## Scope

Applies to scoped GPU clusters and reporting periods. Define applicable device models, telemetry semantics, mapping coverage, provider meters, charge and rate basis, shared costs and missing or delayed data treatment.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Workload and device inventory, monitoring collection, validated identity and time joins, and reconciliation to the authoritative cost export.
- Implementation Surface: Retain workload UID, container, job and team ownership with resource identity and effective time. Validate telemetry joins and billing resource identifiers separately. Reconcile source charges, apply versioned allocation rules, and retain classified totals, unmatched records, adjustments and estimates with their basis.
- Owner Primary: FinOps
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; ML Engineering
