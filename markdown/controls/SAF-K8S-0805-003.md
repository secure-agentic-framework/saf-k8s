# SAF-K8S-0805-003 - GPU anomaly triage and authorized incident response

- Domain: D08 - GPU, Accelerator, and Confidential Computing
- Knowledge Area: 8.5 - GPU Workload Auditing and Monitoring
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Triage suspected accelerator misuse with supported device telemetry, attributable workload and authorization context, and documented evidence thresholds. Require accountable approval for disruptive response unless a specific automated decision is governed, tested and supported by adequate evidence.

## Objective

Detect observable accelerator anomalies, investigate their cause and authorize proportionate response using attributable, retained evidence.

## Rationale

A utilization or health signal describes a hardware condition within the coverage of the collector. A normal authorized workload can exhibit the same condition as suspected misuse. Evidence about device access, workload authorization and incident cause can require different sources, and missing corroboration must remain explicit.

## Scope

Applies to governed accelerator workloads, including managed and shared environments. Document supported signals, applicable hardware and operator versions, workload identity and authorization, investigation handoffs and approval requirements for disruptive response.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Verified telemetry and workload attribution, an owned investigation workflow, and bound authorization for actions affecting workloads or nodes.
- Implementation Surface: Exercise benign high utilization, missing or ambiguous attribution, a corroborated escalation and approved response. Keep distinct decisions for signal receipt, authorization, incident determination and disruption. Retain audit evidence and require safeguards for automatic actions.
- Owner Primary: Security Operations
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; GPU Infrastructure Engineering; Incident Response
