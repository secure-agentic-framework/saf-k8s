# SAF-K8S-0703-023 - Investigation and termination of confirmed unauthorized GPU workloads

- Domain: D07 - Storage, Multi-tenancy, and Resource Governance
- Knowledge Area: 7.3 - Resource Governance and Priority
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Investigate suspected unauthorized accelerator use under the approved incident procedure. When the designated authority confirms the finding and authorizes containment, identify the exact workload and its controller, preserve applicable evidence, take the approved scoped action, and verify the result.

## Objective

Contain confirmed unauthorized resource use while retaining evidence and confirming workload, capacity and service impact.

## Rationale

Containment actions have distinct effects. Controllers can recreate terminated Pods and a quarantine can leave resource use active. Exact identity, prior authorization, evidence preservation and post-action verification reduce disruption and support follow-up analysis.

## Scope

Applies to confirmed incidents within organizational authority. Identify controller ownership, availability and forensic constraints, supported response actions, storage and retention requirements and any separate quarantine or recovery procedures.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Incident authorization and evidence handling, scoped workload actions, and verification of containment or recovery.
- Implementation Surface: Retain evidence of confirmation, exact object identities and the approval record. Determine the controller or operator before acting, use scoped authorization and supported preconditions, and verify workload status, neighboring services and resource disposition. Record remaining forensic and recovery actions separately.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; ML Engineering
