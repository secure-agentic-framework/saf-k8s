# SAF-K8S-0801-011 - MPS and time-slicing residual-risk acceptance and compensating control approval

- Domain: D08 - GPU, Accelerator, and Confidential Computing
- Knowledge Area: 8.1 - GPU Device Plugins and Resource Allocation
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Require an explicit, scoped, current risk decision before permitting sensitive workloads in shared GPU modes. Record the exact deployed mode, documented isolation properties, applicable compensating controls, approver, review period and conditions for revocation.

## Objective

Permit shared accelerator use only within a verified risk acceptance, workload scope and compensating control set.

## Rationale

Time-slicing, MPS and hardware partitioning have different documented resource and isolation properties. Kubernetes resource accounting does not establish the physical sharing mode. Decisions require evidence of actual mode and applicability to the workload trust boundary.

## Scope

Applies to governed NVIDIA MPS, time-slicing and other shared GPU modes requiring a comparable risk decision. Record actual hardware, operator and plugin versions, workload classes, review cadence, exceptions and reassessment triggers.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Operator-owned sharing risk decision, actual deployment evidence, and enforcement of approved scope and current status.
- Implementation Surface: Record the vendor properties, actual pool and mode, affected workloads, owner-approved alternatives, residual risks and required controls. Verify placement, eligibility and current approval, track changes, and revoke or re-review permission when the decision ceases to apply.
- Owner Primary: ML Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; Data Protection Office
