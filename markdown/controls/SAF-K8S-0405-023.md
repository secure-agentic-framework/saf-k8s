# SAF-K8S-0405-023 - Break-glass access audit logging coverage

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.5 - Identity Abuse Detection and Mitigation
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Define and verify audit coverage for governed emergency access. Correlate the activation decision, authenticated principal, protected API operation and outcome. Retain scoped records securely and verify attributable post-use review.

## Objective

Preserve independently reviewable evidence for each approved emergency activation and the operations in its defined scope.

## Rationale

Kubernetes audit events describe requests that reach the API server and match its audit policy. Issuance, provider authentication, collection resilience and post-use review require evidence from their respective systems.

## Scope

Applies to governed emergency paths and operations. Define local API rules and stages, external issuer or access events and retention, reporting ownership and response targets. Verify expiration or withdrawal under SAF-K8S-0405-024.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Effective audit policy and protected collection, correlation and review of observed emergency activity.
- Implementation Surface: Exercise covered and excluded operations, correlate actual identities and outcomes with activation records, minimize sensitive data, and verify retention, event-loss monitoring and review completion across the covered sources.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Identity and Access Management
