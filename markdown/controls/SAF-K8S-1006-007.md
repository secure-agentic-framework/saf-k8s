# SAF-K8S-1006-007 - NIST SSDF v1.1 alignment and gap assessment for Kubernetes AI development

- Domain: D10 - Observability, Incident Response, and Governance
- Knowledge Area: 10.6 - Compliance and Governance
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Organizations must map their Kubernetes and AI/ML development security practices to NIST SSDF v1.1 (SP 800-218) practices and tasks. Document applicability, supporting evidence, and gaps across Prepare the Organization, Protect the Software, Produce Well-Secured Software, and Respond to Vulnerabilities.

## Objective

Show how secure development practices for Kubernetes AI workloads address applicable NIST SSDF recommendations and document gaps for remediation.

## Rationale

Without explicit SSDF alignment and gap assessment, organizations cannot show that their Kubernetes AI development practices address applicable secure development expectations or maintain a consistent baseline across engineering teams and toolchains.

## Scope

Applies to all software development and ML engineering teams building or deploying AI workloads on Kubernetes. Covers the full NIST SSDF v1.1 practice group set and associated baseline secure development gap analysis.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Secure development policy documentation, CI/CD compliance checks, and SSDF self-attestation process.
- Implementation Surface: NIST SSDF v1.1 practice group alignment documentation, gap identification and remediation planning across PO, PS, PW, and RV practice groups, CI/CD checks for applicable SSDF practices, and SSDF self-attestation or assessment workflows.
- Owner Primary: GRC/Compliance
- Owner Supporting: AI/ML Engineering; DevOps/Platform Engineering; Security Architecture
