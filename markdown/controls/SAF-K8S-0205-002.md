# SAF-K8S-0205-002 - NodePort and HostPort restriction policies

- Domain: D02 - Node, Runtime, and OS Security
- Knowledge Area: 2.5 - kube-proxy and Node Networking Security
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Restrict use of Service NodePort allocation and Pod hostPort declarations through separate effective admission rules. Define approved exceptions, compatible exposure alternatives and whether LoadBalancer node-port allocation is allowed. Verify actual reachability separately.

## Objective

Prevent unapproved node-address exposure while preserving authorized connectivity for governed workloads.

## Rationale

NodePort reachability depends on Service mode, node addresses, service proxy, firewall and provider configuration. HostPort introduces a distinct node exposure path whose network enforcement depends on the CNI and node configuration. Pod admission does not validate Service objects.

## Scope

Applies to governed namespaces, Service types and supported Pod paths in production. Record exceptions and verify ownership, effective ingress, alternative exposure and applicable IPv4 and IPv6 paths.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Separate Pod and Service admission. Pod Security Admission can restrict Pod hostPort and a Service validation policy can govern Service type and node-port allocation.
- Implementation Surface: Verify Pod hostPort checks for applicable container types and verify Service create and update decisions, including LoadBalancer allocation, allowed internal Services and scoped exceptions. Check service controller, CNI and firewall evidence for deployed reachability.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Network Engineering; Security Engineering
