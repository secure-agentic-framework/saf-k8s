# SAF-K8S-0405-021 - Break-glass activation multi-party approval enforcement

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.5 - Identity Abuse Detection and Mitigation
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Require separately attributable approval by at least two authorized parties for governed emergency access activation. Enforce the approval decision on the exact intended grant and verify any separately required authentication assurance.

## Objective

Keep one party from unilaterally approving and enabling a governed emergency grant while preserving the authorized scoped recovery operation.

## Rationale

Multiple factors can authenticate one participant. Multiparty authorization requires distinct approved actors and a decision tied to the same request, scope and lifetime.

## Scope

Applies to the inventoried emergency privileges and approved activation paths. Define approver independence and authority, idempotence and expiry, scoped provisioners and evidence for any exceptions. Verify required human MFA separately.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Actual activation authorization that uses independently attributable approvals for the same exact request.
- Implementation Surface: Exercise missing, single, conflicting and valid distinct approvals, authenticated approver attribution, exact target, expiry and activation scope. Test the approved operation and independently verify required MFA and withdrawal.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Identity and Access Management
