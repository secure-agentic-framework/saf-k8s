# SAF-K8S-0201-014 - Kubelet serving certificate trust and expiry enforcement

- Domain: D02 - Node, Runtime, and OS Security
- Knowledge Area: 2.1 - Kubelet and Node Configuration Hardening
- Maturity: Foundational
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Serve a current CA-issued certificate for each scoped kubelet HTTPS endpoint. Require intended clients to verify the trusted CA and matching DNS or IP identity. Renew and verify activation on the endpoint before expiry.

## Objective

Maintain trustworthy kubelet HTTPS endpoints for node management traffic, exec, logs, and metrics access.

## Rationale

Kubelet TLS depends on both issuance and the consumer's verification of the trusted chain, endpoint identity and validity interval. Scoped approval and timely replacement support authentication and reduce interruptions when certificates approach expiry.

## Scope

Applies to all governed kubelet HTTPS endpoints and intended consumers, including those on control-plane nodes. Support Kubernetes serving CSRs, approved external issuers and documented provider lifecycle. Retain evidence of replacement and client trust for each applicable path.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Effective kubelet serving certificate and client verification settings, approved issuance, and expiry monitoring.
- Implementation Surface: For native serving bootstrap, verify the feature support and effective settings, and restrict the explicit serving CSR approver to valid node identities and SANs. For external issuers, verify configured certificate activation. Observe the actual endpoint, monitor time to expiry and pending renewals, and verify trusted client traffic after replacement.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; PKI/Certificate Management
