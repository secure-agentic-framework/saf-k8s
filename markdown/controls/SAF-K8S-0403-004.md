# SAF-K8S-0403-004 - Secret rotation and expiration enforcement

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.3 - Secrets Management
- Maturity: Foundational
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Define and exercise renewal, effective expiry or an approved compensating lifecycle, and revocation for each governed credential class. Verify adoption by the consumer and rejection of retired credentials by the accepting authority.

## Objective

Bound the useful life of governed credentials and verify continuity and retirement across controlled replacement.

## Rationale

Kubernetes Secret objects deliver data. A changed Secret or provider schedule does not itself alter the credentials accepted by a separate service. Leased credentials, static passwords and keys, certificates and Kubernetes tokens have different expiry and revocation properties.

## Scope

Applies to governed credential identities and dependencies. Record trust relationship, issuer and verifier, supported effective lifetime or replacement window, applicable existing-session behavior and approved exceptions.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: The credential issuer and accepting authority, with separately validated delivery, consumer adoption, retirement, and incident procedures.
- Implementation Surface: Record actual credential versions, authoritative validity, receiver behavior, delivery and reload. Exercise missed or failed rotation, scoped overlap, effective expiry or explicit retirement, legacy credential rejection and continued authorized access. Verify termination of existing sessions where required and tool support allows.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Application Development
