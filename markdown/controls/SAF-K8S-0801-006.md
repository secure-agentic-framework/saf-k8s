# SAF-K8S-0801-006 - GPU memory clearing between workload transitions

- Domain: D08 - GPU, Accelerator, and Confidential Computing
- Knowledge Area: 8.1 - GPU Device Plugins and Resource Allocation
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Prevent cross-trust reuse of a GPU allocation until an approved, configuration-specific device or provider sanitization outcome is verified. Where suitable sanitization evidence is unavailable, retain quarantine or use an independently verified isolation or dedicated allocation boundary.

## Objective

Prevent unverified cross-trust reassignment of accelerator resources that could retain prior workload data.

## Rationale

Device release, process teardown, reset and physical memory sanitization have distinct semantics. Kubernetes can enforce reassignment policy. Establish any sanitization or isolated allocation property for the exact device, firmware, driver, virtualization and provider configuration.

## Scope

Applies to accelerator allocation transitions across governed trust or sensitivity boundaries. Include retained allocations and handling of failed, missing, stale or ambiguous evidence and approved dedicated-pool exceptions.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Enforced allocation release and quarantine, backed by configuration-specific approved provider or vendor evidence.
- Implementation Surface: Define the allocation and transition identity, trustworthy evidence producer, verified mechanism and acceptance criteria. Deny reassignment when evidence is missing, stale, mismatched or unsuccessful. Record operating conditions and authorize alternatives such as dedicated allocations when the requested sanitization property cannot be verified.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; AI/ML Engineering
