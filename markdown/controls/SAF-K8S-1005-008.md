# SAF-K8S-1005-008 - AI-specific incident response playbooks for Kubernetes

- Domain: D10 - Observability, Incident Response, and Governance
- Knowledge Area: 10.5 - Incident Response for Kubernetes
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Organizations must develop and maintain AI-specific incident response playbooks for Kubernetes environments covering compromised training jobs, model poisoning detection, GPU cryptomining, and inference endpoint abuse.

## Objective

Ensure that AI workload security incidents have purpose-built response playbooks with specialized detection and containment procedures tailored to AI-specific incident types.

## Rationale

AI workload incidents call for different evidence, authorized containment, and recovery criteria. For suspected training compromise, determine whether to pause the affected work and preserve its inputs and logs. Evaluate suspected model poisoning against approved model behavior and training or artifact provenance. A matching digest establishes consistency with the selected reference bytes. For unexpected GPU use, corroborate device telemetry with workload attribution and approved utilization. For inference endpoint abuse, select the response for the affected route, workload, or account and document when to escalate, contain, or recover.

## Scope

Applies to all Kubernetes clusters hosting AI workloads in production. AI-specific playbooks must address the four named incident types at minimum. Playbooks must be tested through tabletop or live exercises at least annually.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: IR playbook repository, SOC runbook management system, and exercise/drill scheduling process.
- Implementation Surface: Maintain approved playbooks for all four incident types. Identify the authorized responder, the deployed and corroborating signals, the conditions for containment or rollback, and the evidence to persist. Relevant signals can include Kubernetes and pipeline audit events, workload-attributed GPU metrics where available, validated model evaluation or drift indicators, and inference gateway telemetry. Exercise the actual trigger and decision process using approved scenarios.
- Owner Primary: Security Operations
- Owner Supporting: AI/ML Engineering; Platform Engineering; Legal

The portable case validates a mock responder's routing and fixture-only actions for four synthetic scenarios; it does not establish production signal validity, live containment or recovery, GPU/DCGM telemetry, inference protection, or the organization's annual exercise cadence.
