# SAF-K8S-0404-003 - mTLS for service-to-service authentication

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.4 - Certificate Management
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Require mutually authenticated transport for covered service communications. Verify both peer identities under the approved trust, enrollment, expiry and expected-name policies, and enforce separately approved source, destination and operation authorization.

## Objective

Authenticate the service peers and use their verified identities in the effective communication authorization decision.

## Rationale

mTLS establishes authenticated possession of trusted credentials. Endpoint policy determines which authenticated identities may communicate or use an operation. Policy modes and enrollment do not establish live data-path behavior.

## Scope

Applies to communication paths required by the organization, including covered control and data planes. Document protocol and deployment-specific equivalents, exemptions, limitations and approved compensating controls.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Actual peer identity verification and independently enforced service authorization on the selected communication path.
- Implementation Surface: Verify certificate issuance and trust roots, expected service identities, actual bidirectional handshakes, denial of missing or unapproved credentials and an authenticated but unauthorized peer, rotation, residual access paths and policy for designated training traffic.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; Application Development
