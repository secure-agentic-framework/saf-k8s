# SAF-K8S-0908-003 - Model watermarking and fingerprinting

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.8 - Model Abuse and Extraction Prevention
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Where an assessed provenance need warrants output watermarking or fingerprinting, implement an approved marking and detection scheme for eligible serving paths. Document and verify the detector operating criteria, key custody, acceptable quality impact, and supported content transformations.

## Objective

Provide a measured provenance signal for selected generated content with documented detector limitations.

## Rationale

A detector decision indicates consistency with a particular marking scheme under its tested assumptions. Detection reliability varies with content, length, operating threshold and transformations. Attribution and model derivation require independently corroborated evidence.

## Scope

Applies to the eligible production outputs and deployed detection workflows named by the approved provenance plan. Assess model-weights watermarking and signed content provenance separately.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: The actual serving output marker and approved detector workflow.
- Implementation Surface: Define the governed output and detector version, representative positive and negative corpora, measured detection and false-positive rates, approved transformations and quality criteria, keys and audit. Record unresolved detection scope and separate provenance evidence.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; Legal
