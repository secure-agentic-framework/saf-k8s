# SAF-K8S-0910-021 - Cross-cluster endpoint and workload authentication for AI communication

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.10 - Multi-Cluster and Federated AI Security
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Authenticate the exact workload or cluster endpoint identities for governed cross-cluster connections. Validate the expected name or workload identifier, credential validity and appropriate issuer and trust domain at the accepting components.

## Objective

Admit only the expected endpoint and peer identities on governed cross-cluster communications.

## Rationale

Transport encryption and a trusted issuer alone do not establish the exact service that connects. Validate the intended identity for each direction and scope its authorization separately.

## Scope

Applies to governed workload and cluster links. Verify identity issuance and validation, trust relationships, names, session lifetime, renewal and failure behavior for actual endpoints.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Effective peer identity validation at actual service, mesh or transport termination points.
- Implementation Surface: Record expected endpoint identities, approved trust roots and namespaces, peer matching and certificate lifecycle. Verify refusal of another eligible issuer identity and an unexpected destination where relevant.
- Owner Primary: Network Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Security Engineering; ML Platform Engineering
