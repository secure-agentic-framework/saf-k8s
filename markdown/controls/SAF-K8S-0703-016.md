# SAF-K8S-0703-016 - AI workload PriorityClass hierarchy and preemption protection

- Domain: D07 - Storage, Multi-tenancy, and Resource Governance
- Knowledge Area: 7.3 - Resource Governance and Priority
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Define and govern PriorityClasses for the applicable workload tiers. Select the allowed preemption behavior for each tier. A higher-priority Pod with PreemptLowerPriority may preempt eligible lower-priority Pods when it makes scheduling feasible. A Pod with Never cannot initiate preemption and remains eligible to be preempted by a higher-priority Pod.

## Objective

Make scheduling priority and preemption decisions explicit and separately verify the controls needed for inference availability.

## Rationale

Priority orders eligible pending work and can make lower-priority Pods eligible for preemption. PodDisruptionBudget consideration during preemption is best effort. Availability also depends on reserved or available capacity, placement, admission controls, replicas and recovery.

## Scope

Applies to governed scheduling tiers for inference, training, development and any protected platform workloads. Validate actual GPU admission and availability on the applicable accelerator-backed deployment.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: PriorityClass definitions, Pod admission and scheduling policy, with separate serving continuity controls.
- Implementation Surface: Document approved numeric priorities and preemption behavior for each tier. Verify actual scheduler decisions under representative contention and enforce who may use each tier. Protect the serving capacity objective with the identified topology, capacity and disruption controls, and collect operating and recovery evidence separately.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Engineering; FinOps
