# SAF-K8S-0703-019 - GPU chargeback and showback reporting accountability

- Domain: D07 - Storage, Multi-tenancy, and Resource Governance
- Knowledge Area: 7.3 - Resource Governance and Priority
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Produce recurring GPU cost allocation reports for accountable owners and verify their availability and periodic review. Distinguish informational showback from any approved financial chargeback, which is carried out separately under Finance and Accounting policy.

## Objective

Give responsible owners an auditable view of GPU cost allocations, assumptions and exceptions, and document the review or financial action where required.

## Rationale

A reproducible allocation report can inform cost review and capacity decisions. Report generation, authorized distribution, documented owner review and financial posting each require evidence appropriate to their separate decisions.

## Scope

Applies to governed shared GPU workloads and reporting periods. Define the owner and rate sources, cost and currency basis, shared and idle amounts, exceptions and cadence. A financial posting is in scope only when selected through approved policy.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: FinOps reporting and scoped distribution, documented accountable review, and authorized financial workflows where applicable.
- Implementation Surface: Retain versioned usage, ownership and pricing inputs and reproducible report totals. Disclose shared, unallocated, unpriced and disputed records. Verify access by intended recipients and retain review evidence and remediation records separately. Reconcile and authorize any financial posting through the approved Finance and Accounting process.
- Owner Primary: FinOps
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; ML Engineering
