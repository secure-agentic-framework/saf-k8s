# SAF-K8S-0910-019 - Compromised-cluster federation isolation and re-admission governance

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.10 - Multi-Cluster and Federated AI Security
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Define an approved circuit-breaker workflow for a governed member cluster that requires isolation. Prevent new placements, revoke applicable access and handle existing workloads as the policy requires. Require independently authorized readmission tied to the exact member and isolation decision.

## Objective

Contain an affected member and restore only approved federation functions following verified remediation.

## Rationale

Placement-time screening cannot guarantee ongoing trust. Isolation and readmission require exact member identity, measured effect, explicit restoration and written approval.

## Scope

Applies to governed member clusters and existing orchestration paths. Verify signal authority, isolation delivery, drain and continuing workload exceptions, and recovery policy in the actual deployment.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Federation membership and placement authorization, active-workload containment and independent readmission approval.
- Implementation Surface: Record the triggering decision and exact member identity. Verify new placement denial and documented runtime handling. Require authenticated independent approval and reassess the member before restoring least-privilege access.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; Security Engineering; SRE
