# SAF-K8S-0908-008 - Inference output perturbation and privacy-preserving response shaping

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.8 - Model Abuse and Extraction Prevention
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Select and validate a response shaping or perturbation policy where detailed inference outputs present an assessed disclosure risk. Verify the effective response schema or transform and measure utility under approved criteria.

## Objective

Reduce exposure of governed response detail while preserving approved prediction utility.

## Rationale

Response transforms can suppress or coarsen selected information. Their real disclosure protection depends on the mechanism, supported routes, attacker assumptions and permitted repeated use. Formal differential privacy requires independently verified evidence.

## Scope

Applies to governed inference response paths that expose protected detail. Define output classes, transformation, legitimate uses and measurable utility. Use SAF-K8S-0908-009 for a formal privacy claim and SAF-K8S-0908-010 where releases consume cumulative privacy budget.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Observed transformation and release contract at the served inference response.
- Implementation Surface: Test shaped schema and precision, preserved required semantics or approved utility, legacy and alternate model routes, failure behavior and evaluated actual leakage risk.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; Privacy Engineering
