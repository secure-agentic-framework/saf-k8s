# SAF-K8S-0403-021 - Automated AI workload credential inventory

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.3 - Secrets Management
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Reconcile non-secret workload credential metadata for the owner-approved issuer inventory within a defined freshness objective. Mark unavailable, denied, stale or unsupported source coverage explicitly.

## Objective

Identify which governed workload credentials are issuer-valid, who owns them, and where active state cannot be verified.

## Rationale

Issuer records, leases, issuance events and workload binding declarations provide different views. Passing an inventory query supports only its registered source scope. Kubernetes projected credentials are not enumerated by listing long-lived Secret objects.

## Scope

Applies to registered workload credential issuers and managed projections across governed platforms and workloads. Record collection prerequisites, authority and ownership boundaries, and unmanaged or unsupported cases.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Scoped issuer collectors, stable identity reconciliation and freshness monitoring with protected inventory metadata.
- Implementation Surface: Register supported Kubernetes, Vault, identity-provider and external-service sources by credential type. Reconcile issuer and workload identity, scope and lifecycle metadata without storing credential values. Verify at defined intervals and expose incomplete, stale or uncollected sources separately from confirmed zero credentials.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; ML Engineering; Cloud Platform Engineering
