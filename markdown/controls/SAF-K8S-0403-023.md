# SAF-K8S-0403-023 - Credential scope drift monitoring for AI workloads

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.3 - Secrets Management
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Compare effective workload grants with approved intent, time-bounded exceptions, and attributable usage within explicitly covered observation windows. Route unapproved changes or candidate unused grants for distinct decisions and preserve unknown usage when telemetry is incomplete.

## Objective

Detect newly effective permissions outside approved scope and enable evidence-based review of existing and exception grants.

## Rationale

Identity credentials, effective authorization and usage events answer different questions. An unapproved grant can exist without being used. Unobserved use can reflect a standby requirement or a source gap.

## Scope

Applies to governed AI and ML workload identities where grants can be determined. Identify unsupported issuer and usage sources explicitly. Credential lifecycle metrics remain governed by SAF-K8S-0403-024.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Effective authorization snapshots and approved baselines, attributable usage collection, and an owner-reviewed grant and exception workflow.
- Implementation Surface: Inventory entitlement sources, workload bindings, approved exceptions and review dates. Check effective current authorization and grant changes separately from observed use, with a recorded window and telemetry health. Verify the result of approved permission changes.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; ML Engineering; Cloud Platform Engineering
