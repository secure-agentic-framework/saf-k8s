# SAF-K8S-0804-004 - Confidential AI workload operational constraints and risk assessment

- Domain: D08 - GPU, Accelerator, and Confidential Computing
- Knowledge Area: 8.4 - Confidential Computing for AI Workloads
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Maintain a workload-specific assessment of confidential computing readiness, supported platform combinations, measured capacity and performance, operational recovery and residual risk. Record an approved adoption or deferral decision with compensating controls and reassessment criteria.

## Objective

Base confidential workload adoption and deferral on observed workload requirements and supported deployment evidence.

## Rationale

Capacity, scheduling and performance effects depend on the exact workload and platform. Declared Kubernetes overhead affects policy and scheduling while actual confidential computing overhead requires measurement on the deployed stack. Confirm operational dependencies and determine which residual risks remain.

## Scope

Applies to confidential AI deployments and evaluations. Cover versioned infrastructure, representative training and inference profiles, baseline and protected posture, evidence dependencies, operational and resource limits, and approved deferrals.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Maintained workload qualification evidence, approved risk and capacity decisions, and review of measured deployment outcomes.
- Implementation Surface: Verify supported combinations and effective runtime, measure representative workloads with a reproducible baseline and agreed metrics, and document quotas, readiness, upgrades and recovery. Record residual risk, exceptions and compensating controls and repeat after relevant changes.
- Owner Primary: ML Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: GPU Infrastructure Engineering; Security Engineering; Architecture Review Board
