# SAF-K8S-0303-004 - MAC profile generation from runtime behavior

- Domain: D03 - Workload and Pod Security
- Knowledge Area: 3.3 - Mandatory Access Controls
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

For governed custom workload profiles, require an evidence-backed candidate and review its approved behavior against the applicable enforcement mechanism. Use runtime recording when supported and representative, and review observed operations before deriving or changing policy.

## Objective

Produce a scoped candidate that protects the approved workload and preserves verified operations under the actual enforcement mechanism.

## Rationale

Recorders see only supported event types and exercised workload paths. Observed operations can be incidental or overbroad, and legitimate recovery paths can be absent. Recording informs candidate generation and complements reviewed policy authoring and direct enforcement checks.

## Scope

Applies to creating or updating governed custom seccomp, AppArmor and SELinux policies. Define actual tool and platform support, observed event classes, workload and dependency revisions, representative normal and recovery paths, and collection limits.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Candidate policy authoring, source and evidence review, compatible staging, and verified final enforcement.
- Implementation Surface: Pin the collector, relevant kernel and runtime versions, workload coverage and exact outputs. Verify expected events and identify gaps, permit only operations approved for the workload, review generated or curated changes, and test allowed and denied behavior and rollback under enforcement.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Application Development teams
