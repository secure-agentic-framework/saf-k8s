# SAF-K8S-0403-028 - Exposed credential revocation and replacement execution

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.3 - Secrets Management
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Invalidate exposed credentials at their enforcing authority within an organization-defined, risk-based response window. Issue and deploy a scoped replacement when legitimate use continues, and verify old denial and continued authorized operation.

## Objective

Remove the exposed credential's effective access on time and restore authorized use only where it remains approved.

## Rationale

Removing the leaked copy does not establish issuer-side invalidation. Credential disposal, required replacement, relying-party propagation and actual authorization decisions need distinct evidence.

## Scope

Applies to credentials covered by the incident and issuer inventories. Classify authority-side invalidation or containment mechanisms and propagation constraints. Link triage and containment under SAF-K8S-0403-027.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Authorized issuer-side lifecycle action, observed old-credential denial and applicable replacement verification.
- Implementation Surface: Capture finding time, authorized operator, issuer reference without credential values, defined deadline, action and effective denial. Qualify residual-validity windows and separately verify any replacement and the legitimate consumer's successful operation.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Application Development
