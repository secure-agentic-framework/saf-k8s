# SAF-K8S-0403-027 - Secret leak incident triage and containment workflow

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.3 - Secrets Management
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Maintain and exercise a governed workflow for credential-leak findings. Verify and prioritize each report, assign an accountable responder, use authorized containment actions, escalate under the defined response policy and retain observed decisions and timestamps.

## Objective

Determine whether a valid finding reached the right owner and whether the selected containment took effect within the organization-defined target.

## Rationale

A finding, a routed escalation and an effective containment action supply distinct evidence. Record unresolved decisions and link provider-side credential lifecycle work under SAF-K8S-0403-028.

## Scope

Applies to governed development, artifact, log and runtime exposure reports. Define intake and alert sources, ownership, escalation and isolation actions appropriate to each exposure surface.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Authorized response workflow, confirmation and prioritization records, escalation handoff, and verification of resulting containment.
- Implementation Surface: Exercise a public fixture finding through intake, confirmation, ownership, escalation, and a bounded containment action. Verify the affected path is contained, necessary unaffected access stays available, and the record preserves timing, actions and handoffs.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Application Development
