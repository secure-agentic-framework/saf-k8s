# SAF-K8S-0404-010 - Post-compromise certificate recovery validation

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.4 - Certificate Management
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Before closing a governed certificate compromise response, verify that affected service endpoints and dependent clients operate on the intended replacement certificate and key. Exercise authenticated traffic, required workflows, trust boundaries and applicable rejection of stale credentials.

## Objective

Verify restoration of the approved live service path and operation following certificate invalidation and replacement.

## Rationale

An issued certificate, a changed Secret and a healthy process do not establish that the endpoint presents the replacement or that relying clients can validate it and use the intended operation.

## Scope

Applies to affected control-plane and workload certificate paths and dependent consumers. Record effective path, fallback and recovery thresholds. Issuer revocation and reissuance are covered by SAF-K8S-0404-009.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Observed live certificate identity, chain and relying-client function under the prescribed recovery procedure.
- Implementation Surface: Check each applicable active endpoint and consuming client using the intended replacement identity and trust. Exercise critical traffic and fallback paths, check residual use of prior credentials or stale endpoints, record measured recovery and preserve independent service operation.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; Security Operations
