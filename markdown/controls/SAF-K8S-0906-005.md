# SAF-K8S-0906-005 - Annotation workflow integrity and label-shift review

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.6 - Training Data Integrity and Poisoning Defense
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Authenticate annotation contributors and bind each review to an exact dataset version and asset. Require independent approval or authorized adjudication, and hold unresolved records before training. Use agreement and historical label metrics to prioritize independent quality investigations.

## Objective

Prevent unapproved or unverifiable annotations from entering training and identify changes that require review.

## Rationale

Version and actor checks verify custody and review policy. Agreement and distribution checks summarize observed changes and require separate evidence to assess semantic correctness or intent.

## Scope

Applies to governed human and automated annotation inputs, corrections and release workflows. Verify actual identity, labels, review policy, versioned storage, and monitoring accuracy and follow-up.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Annotation access controls, independently authorized workflow decisions and the label release gate.
- Implementation Surface: Record exact dataset and asset identities, trusted reviewers and adjudication, measure agreement and distribution changes, retain review history and block releases with missing, inconsistent or unresolved labels.
- Owner Primary: Data Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Security Engineering; ML Engineering
