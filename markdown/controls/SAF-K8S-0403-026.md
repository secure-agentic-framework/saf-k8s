# SAF-K8S-0403-026 - Secret leak prevention gate and enforcement controls

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.3 - Secrets Management
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Require an effective, automated secret detection gate on each approved publication transition where blocking is required. Verify that supported findings prevent the named commit, push, merge, release or deployment action and record approved exceptions and other paths.

## Objective

Prevent publication at the actual governed boundary for covered credential indicators while preserving approved benign publication.

## Rationale

Local Git checks, server receive protection, mandatory pipeline checks and deployment admission provide different guarantees. A check can detect a finding without controlling the requested transition.

## Scope

Applies to approved governed development, release and deployment transitions and their applicable credential rules. Separate prevention from broader detection and incident response.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Required policy or hook at the named publication transition with observable state preservation on denial.
- Implementation Surface: Inventory required transitions, detector rules, protected paths and limitations. Verify supported public synthetic indicators are blocked, rejected state remains unchanged, and benign input passes. Record review and authorization of covered exceptions and platform limits.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Application Development
