# SAF-K8S-0405-012 - Privileged MFA enforcement for cluster administration

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.5 - Identity Abuse Detection and Mitigation
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Enforce the approved multifactor assurance policy on all defined privileged human access paths to Kubernetes clusters and AI infrastructure. Verify actual provider challenges and resulting privileged access. Record separately governed workload identities and emergency access exceptions.

## Objective

Prevent a single compromised primary credential from granting governed human administrative access and verify the enforcement and assurance of each covered path.

## Rationale

Cluster access may rely on upstream identity-provider or cloud assurance. Kubernetes role assignment, a configured issuer or a fixture assertion does not establish the factor the human completed. Effective protection depends on the authenticator type and every available path.

## Scope

Applies to designated privileged human access including covered federation, cloud administration and direct or fallback credentials. Inventory the pathway, provider and required assurance. Review non-interactive workload access and emergency paths with their distinct approvals and safeguards.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Effective multifactor policy at the authoritative verifier and verified assurance at the relying privileged access boundary.
- Implementation Surface: Exercise valid primary credentials without an approved second factor, authorized use after actual factor completion, and separately governed fallback or emergency paths. Verify authoritative provider events, issued identity assurance and Kubernetes or cloud effective access. Verify phishing resistance where required by the applicable assurance policy.
- Owner Primary: Identity and Access Management
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Security Engineering
