# SAF-K8S-0405-018 - Secure credential storage and lifecycle governance

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.5 - Identity Abuse Detection and Mitigation
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Protect governed credential material at rest and enforce each credential class's approved lifecycle. Identify the accountable issuer, storage and relying verifier. Verify actual storage protection, least-privilege access, replacement and the rejection of retired credentials where applicable.

## Objective

Reduce credential exposure and ensure that governed replacements and retirement take effect at the actual enforcement boundaries.

## Rationale

Secret encoding is distinct from encryption, and ciphertext does not by itself limit which API callers can read a Secret. Credential replacement and retirement depend on the actual issuer and relying authentication or authorization mechanism.

## Scope

Applies to inventoried administrative and workload credentials for Kubernetes and governed AI infrastructure, including credentials maintained by external identity and cloud providers. Identify approved storage, issuance, lifetime, replacement and retirement for each. See SAF-K8S-0405-017 for human password selection policy.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Observed protection at the storage provider, and verified lifecycle controls at the issuer and relying boundary.
- Implementation Surface: Verify encrypted or otherwise approved storage and protected keys and backups, named permissions, actual credential issuance and replacement, relying-verifier rejection of retired material, independent approved access and retained audit evidence.
- Owner Primary: Identity and Access Management
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Security Engineering
