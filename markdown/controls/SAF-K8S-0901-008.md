# SAF-K8S-0901-008 - Training job network isolation

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.1 - Distributed Training Workload Security
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Distributed training jobs must implement documented network-level isolation controls (Kubernetes NetworkPolicy, fabric segmentation, dedicated VLANs) to restrict inter-worker communication to authorized pod selectors and prevent lateral movement from training namespaces, particularly where native transport encryption is not practical.

## Objective

Prevent unauthorized network access to distributed training communication paths and contain lateral movement from compromised training workers through network-level segmentation.

## Rationale

Not all communication fabrics support native encryption -- RDMA over InfiniBand and some NCCL transport paths have limited or no encryption support. Network-level isolation controls are required as compensating controls where transport-layer encryption is not feasible, and as defense-in-depth even where encryption is enabled.

## Scope

Applies to all multi-node distributed training workloads on Kubernetes. Covers Kubernetes NetworkPolicy for training namespaces, InfiniBand partition key (P_Key) configuration, and dedicated VLAN or fabric segmentation for training traffic. Required as primary control where native transport encryption is not supported and as defense-in-depth where it is.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Kubernetes NetworkPolicy enforcement for training namespaces and fabric-level segmentation configuration.
- Implementation Surface: Kubernetes NetworkPolicy resources with job-specific label selectors, InfiniBand partition key (P_Key) configuration, dedicated training VLANs, fabric segmentation configuration, and network scan results confirming isolation.
- Owner Primary: Network Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Platform Engineering; Security Engineering
