# SAF-K8S-0801-012 - MPS and time-slicing workload eligibility and same-trust co-location enforcement

- Domain: D08 - GPU, Accelerator, and Confidential Computing
- Knowledge Area: 8.1 - GPU Device Plugins and Resource Allocation
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Restrict approved NVIDIA MPS or time-slicing pools to workloads with a compatible recorded trust classification. Enforce eligibility and placement through the deployed admission, scheduler and resource allocation paths.

## Objective

Prevent workloads outside the approved trust boundary from being admitted to or allocated resources in a shared accelerator pool.

## Rationale

A kubelet shared-resource advertisement reports scheduling capacity. It does not establish that workloads are mutually trusted or that hardware isolation meets the workload requirement. Treat sharing mode, workload eligibility and actual placement as separate facts.

## Scope

Applies to governed accelerator sharing pools and each supported submission, scheduling and allocation path. Inventory workload-controller identities, resource names, pool modes, trust assignments and approved exceptions.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Effective submission admission, scheduler placement constraints and deployed sharing mode and allocation checks.
- Implementation Surface: Define protected pool labels and trust rosters. Enforce eligible authenticated submitters or verified workload ownership and required placement. Review node assignment and any alternate scheduling paths. Verify the installed plugin mode and actual allocations and keep sensitive workloads on approved dedicated or protected resources.
