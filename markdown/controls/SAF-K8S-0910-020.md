# SAF-K8S-0910-020 - Cross-cluster transport encryption for distributed AI traffic

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.10 - Multi-Cluster and Federated AI Security
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Require approved encryption and integrity protection across every governed cross-cluster AI data path. Define required transport modes and terminate encryption only at documented trusted boundaries.

## Objective

Protect cross-cluster transfers from unauthorized observation or alteration along the actual data routes.

## Rationale

A protected hop proves its specific session and keying. Separately verify all routed, fallback and forwarded paths and any intermediate termination.

## Scope

Applies to governed cross-cluster training, serving and data pipeline transports. Verify algorithms and versions, routing and fallback, intermediary termination, and operational scope independently from workload authorization.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Effective transport policy and configuration at source, destination and any approved intermediary.
- Implementation Surface: Inventory paths, set a protocol and cryptographic baseline, deny cleartext fallback where required and retain negotiated and end-to-end observations from the actual routes.
- Owner Primary: Network Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Security Engineering; ML Platform Engineering
