# SAF-K8S-0701-004 - PV reclaim policy enforcement for AI data volumes

- Domain: D07 - Storage, Multi-tenancy, and Resource Governance
- Knowledge Area: 7.1 - Persistent Storage Security
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Organizations must set reclaim policies according to the data lifecycle of their PersistentVolumes. Enforce Retain for training data backups and model checkpoint archives. Verify storage sanitization or controlled disposal separately before reusing or destroying a retained asset.

## Objective

Prevent unintended data loss of valuable AI artifacts through premature volume deletion and prevent data leakage through insecure volume reclamation.

## Rationale

Retain leaves a released PersistentVolume and preserves its associated data for manual reclamation. Storage supporting Delete can remove the PersistentVolume and the associated storage asset through its provisioner. Verify deletion and sanitization with the storage backend. Require independent authorization for destruction of retained backups and archives.

## Scope

Applies to all persistent volumes in Kubernetes clusters hosting AI workloads, with mandatory Retain policy for volumes storing training data backups and model checkpoint archives. Dual authorization requirements apply to deletion requests for retained AI data volumes. Scratch volumes and ephemeral training caches may use Delete policy with documented justification.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: PersistentVolume reclaim policies, StorageClass defaults, and admission policies requiring Retain for classified AI data claims. Independently verify the storage destruction and sanitization workflow.
- Implementation Surface: PV spec.persistentVolumeReclaimPolicy, StorageClass reclaimPolicy defaults, approved storage classes, classification labels and admission rules. Check the provisioner lifecycle, retention and recoverability, documented independent destruction approvals, and storage provider sanitization evidence.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: ML Engineering; Security Engineering; Data Governance

[Kubernetes PersistentVolume reclaiming](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/storage/persistent-volumes/#reclaiming).
