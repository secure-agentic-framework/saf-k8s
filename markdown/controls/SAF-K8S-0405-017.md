# SAF-K8S-0405-017 - Credential policy baseline requirements

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.5 - Identity Abuse Detection and Mitigation
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Define and verify the applicable human password policy at each actual password verifier used for governed Kubernetes or AI infrastructure access. Identify the governing standard and separately verify credential class requirements for paths that do not use passwords.

## Objective

Require appropriate password selection and verifier safeguards with evidence of effective enforcement on each governed human password path.

## Rationale

Password policy is enforced by the actual verifier, which may be upstream of a federated Kubernetes or cloud access path. Password length, common and compromised value checks and failed-attempt protection are distinct from storage, authenticator lifecycle and multifactor requirements.

## Scope

Applies to governed human password verifiers and their integration paths. Inventory the accountable verifier, applicable policy and covered subjects. Evaluate non-password tokens, certificates and workload credentials under applicable mechanism-specific controls. See SAF-K8S-0405-018 for managed credential storage and lifecycle.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Effective human password policy at the accountable identity provider or other covered verifier.
- Implementation Surface: Verify the governing rules and observed acceptance and rejection of candidate password changes at the proper verifier. Where NIST SP 800-63B-4 applies, verify password minimum length, an approved blocklist and required effective rate limiting, without imposing character-class composition requirements. Verify actual policy coverage or provider-specific exceptions separately.
- Owner Primary: Identity and Access Management
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Security Engineering
