# SAF-K8S-0805-001 - GPU telemetry collection and anomaly detection

- Domain: D08 - GPU, Accelerator, and Confidential Computing
- Knowledge Area: 8.5 - GPU Workload Auditing and Monitoring
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Collect supported accelerator telemetry for governed workloads and verify coverage, freshness, and configured anomaly alerts. Record unsupported signals and coverage gaps. Investigate operational and security hypotheses using corroborating evidence.

## Objective

Detect configured accelerator anomalies and visibility failures and give responders attributable, actionable signals.

## Rationale

Available metrics depend on the device, driver, partition or sharing mode, collection stack and provider. Accelerator signals show observed conditions. Determining the cause or authorization of a workload requires independent context.

## Scope

Applies to governed accelerator environments, including managed services. Inventory each target, supported metric and observed scope, partition and workload attribution, sample timing, alert owners and organization-approved blind spots.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Supported exporter or provider telemetry source, effective scrape and retention configuration, and tested alert and response routes.
- Implementation Surface: Select meaningful utilization, memory, temperature, power, error or throttling metrics as supported. Verify the identity and availability of each monitored target and series, alert on missing or stale signals, and exercise alert firing and routing. Reconcile observations with workload inventory and baselines before attributing incidents.
- Owner Primary: ML Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Observability Engineering; Security Operations; GPU Infrastructure Engineering
