# SAF-K8S-1007-011 - Kubernetes cluster upgrade planning, sequencing, and rollback governance

- Domain: D10 - Observability, Incident Response, and Governance
- Knowledge Area: 10.7 - Cluster Lifecycle and Asset Inventory
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Organizations must maintain documented Kubernetes upgrade plans that enforce supported version skew and component sequencing. For control-plane, node and add-on changes affecting AI workloads, rehearse the applicable recovery path and document which rollback, replacement or restore operations the distribution supports.

## Objective

Reduce service disruption and security regression during upgrades through version-skew discipline, ordered sequencing and a tested component-specific recovery plan.

## Rationale

Unsupported version combinations, incorrect sequencing and untested recovery increase the risk of partial upgrades and prolonged outages. Workload rollback, node replacement and control-plane or data restoration address different failure modes.

## Scope

Applies to Kubernetes clusters hosting AI workloads, including control plane, worker node, and cluster add-on upgrades managed directly or through managed-service coordination.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Cluster lifecycle management tooling, change management approval process, and upgrade testing pipeline.
- Implementation Surface: Verify the applicable skew policy and effective component versions at each stage, rehearse disruption-aware worker maintenance and health gates, and test compatible add-on and workload recovery. Follow the approved distribution or provider procedure for control-plane recovery. Use SAF-K8S-0102-008 and SAF-K8S-1005-030 when recovery depends on etcd backups.
- Owner Primary: Platform Engineering
- Owner Supporting: AI/ML Engineering; Security Engineering; SRE
