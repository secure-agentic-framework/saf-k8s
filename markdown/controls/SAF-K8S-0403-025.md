# SAF-K8S-0403-025 - Automated secret leak detection coverage across development and runtime surfaces

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.3 - Secrets Management
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Define automated secret detection and alert routing for each applicable repository, image, build artifact, workload log source and centralized log platform. Test each surface against its declared rule coverage and detection and notification targets.

## Objective

Verify separately that covered content is scanned and findings reach the expected owner within the defined operating window.

## Rationale

A repository finding does not demonstrate that image layers, retained artifacts or live logs are examined. Coverage and latency depend on configured scanners, supported formats and event and alert delivery.

## Scope

Applies to governed development and runtime surfaces that may carry credentials. Identify any format, asset, tenant and source gaps and exceptions. Preventive blocking and incident response are separately governed.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Asset-specific detector configuration, coverage tests, alert route and source-attributable findings.
- Implementation Surface: Inventory sources, content formats, rules, exclusions, trigger timing and expected recipients. Prove each applicable surface detects a case-owned public marker and continues to handle a benign control, and verify timely delivery and source coverage.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Application Development
