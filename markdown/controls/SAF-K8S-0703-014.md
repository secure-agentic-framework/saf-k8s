# SAF-K8S-0703-014 - EDoS spending guardrails and autoscaling limits for AI resources

- Domain: D07 - Storage, Multi-tenancy, and Resource Governance
- Knowledge Area: 7.3 - Resource Governance and Priority
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Define and verify enforced autoscaling maxima and applicable GPU and compute resource limits for cloud-hosted AI workloads. Configure provider spending monitoring and scoped automated responses whose triggers, scope, and effective behavior are verified.

## Objective

Prevent uncontrolled cloud spending and resource amplification from AI workload auto-scaling by enforcing explicit economic guardrails on GPU and cloud resource consumption.

## Rationale

Faulty or abusive workloads can rapidly increase allocation and cost. Enforced resource and autoscaling limits constrain allocation. A budget alert reports measured or forecast spend and may arrive after costs accrue. An enforced spend cap or automated response depends on supported service, account, timing, permissions and action scope.

## Scope

Applies to cloud-hosted Kubernetes clusters with auto-scaling enabled for AI workloads. Covers cloud spending guardrails, auto-scaling limits, queue and quota reactions tied to spend controls, and enforcement workflows for GPU-backed AI infrastructure.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Cloud auto-scaling maximum limits and spending guardrails with automatic enforcement.
- Implementation Surface: Verify provider and Kubernetes autoscaling maxima and applicable compute quotas. Distinguish alert thresholds from independently enforced spend caps where supported. Define authenticated event inputs, freshness and metering delays, constrained action permissions, safe scale-down or Job suspension, escalation, rollback and evidence for the approved scope.
- Owner Primary: SRE
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Security Engineering; FinOps
