# SAF-K8S-1006-009 - Policy decision evidence and export

- Domain: D10 - Observability, Incident Response, and Governance
- Knowledge Area: 10.6 - Compliance and Governance
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Define and operate evidence collection for in-scope Kubernetes policy paths. Inventory policies, admission decisions, and resource classes, including AI workload and policy classes where deployed. Capture outcomes from sources that can observe them. Record the event time, cluster, affected resource, policy and rule, outcome, and a trusted revision or content digest matched to the policy actually applied. Assign a review cadence and maximum export delay. Protect retained evidence against unauthorized changes, detect delivery failures, preserve unacknowledged records for retry or reconciliation, and report unresolved gaps.

## Objective

Produce audit-ready evidence of policy enforcement outcomes so compliance and control effectiveness can be demonstrated without manual reconstruction.

## Rationale

A policy report describes evaluated resource state and may disappear when the resource is deleted. Admission audit coverage depends on the configured recording scope and export behavior. A source commit alone cannot establish the live policy at decision time. Correlating applied policy, policy reports and admission records supports accountability and helps detect evidence gaps.

## Scope

Applies to in-scope policy-enforced Kubernetes workloads and their evidence pipelines. Include AI resource classes and their applicable policies in the inventory where deployed. Apply the organization's retention, integrity and delivery requirements, including SAF-K8S-1001-024, SAF-K8S-1001-026, SAF-K8S-1001-033, SAF-K8S-1001-034 and SAF-K8S-1001-038 where applicable.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Policy reporting pipelines, admission decision logs, and evidence export integrations.
- Implementation Surface: Policy reports or scans for resource evaluations and Kubernetes audit or engine decision events for admission. Compare approved source with the live policy and correlate that evidence to distinct decision records. Run a periodic coverage and lag check, minimize recorded payloads, and use protected evidence storage with acknowledgments, retries or reconciliation, and gap alerts.
- Owner Primary: GRC/Compliance
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; AI/ML Engineering; Security Engineering
