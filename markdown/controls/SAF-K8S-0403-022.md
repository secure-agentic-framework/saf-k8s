# SAF-K8S-0403-022 - Orphaned AI workload credential detection and remediation

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.3 - Secrets Management
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Reconcile governed credentials against durable workload lifecycle evidence at a defined cadence. Use immutable workload identities and verify issuer-side retirement or the specifically approved expiry behavior. Keep unresolved identity and provider failures visible.

## Objective

Remove authorized credential access for terminal workloads within approved limits and preserve credentials belonging to active or replacement workloads.

## Rationale

Terminal workload conditions do not necessarily change provider-issued credentials or delete a bound Pod. Some issuers support revocation while other credentials require permission withdrawal or bounded expiry. A deleted inventory record does not prove access was withdrawn.

## Scope

Applies to governed workload credential classes after completion, failure, deletion or another approved terminal condition. Include reusable workload names, stable instance identity and provider-specific lifecycle limits.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Durable workload-to-credential identity reconciliation, scoped provider remediation and independent verification of resulting authorization.
- Implementation Surface: Record immutable identity, issuer, evidence of terminal state, and class-specific deadlines and allowed actions. Test successful and failed workloads, same-name replacement, missing evidence, denied or delayed provider action, and confirmed loss of access or bounded expiry. Escalate unresolved cases within the defined service level.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; ML Engineering; Cloud Platform Engineering
