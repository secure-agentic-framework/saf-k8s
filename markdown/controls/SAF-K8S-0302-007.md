# SAF-K8S-0302-007 - Safe fsGroup and supplementalGroups volume ownership

- Domain: D03 - Workload and Pod Security
- Knowledge Area: 3.2 - Security Contexts and Capabilities
- Maturity: Foundational
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Pods that use fsGroup or supplementalGroups must restrict assigned groups to approved workload identities. Limit fsGroup-driven ownership or permission changes to designated workload volumes and prevent the resulting process identity from writing sensitive paths, shared platform volumes, or host-backed filesystems outside its approved storage scope.

## Objective

Prevent filesystem group ownership settings from creating cross-workload tampering paths or unintended write access to sensitive storage.

## Rationale

Both settings can affect a container process group identity. fsGroup can also affect group ownership and permissions on supported mounted volumes, either through the kubelet or through a supporting storage driver. supplementalGroups adds process group memberships. Existing filesystem permissions may grant those groups access even when ownership is unchanged.

## Scope

Applies to workloads that mount persistent volumes, shared datasets, model caches, or other writable storage in production clusters.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Admission policy rules and workload validation that restrict approved fsGroup and supplementalGroups use for mounted storage.
- Implementation Surface: Approved fsGroup and supplementalGroups settings, the effective process groups, volume and mount policy, storage-driver behavior, and observed permissions on approved and protected storage.
- Owner Primary: Application Development teams
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Security Engineering
