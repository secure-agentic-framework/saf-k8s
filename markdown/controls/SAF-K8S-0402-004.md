# SAF-K8S-0402-004 - Workload identity attribute integrity

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.2 - Service Accounts and Workload Identity
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Organizations must maintain accurate and protected identity attributes for workload identities, including service account annotations, labels, and namespace bindings, and must prevent unauthorized modification of these attributes.

## Objective

Ensure that workload identity metadata remains trustworthy and cannot be tampered with to bypass authorization decisions that rely on identity attributes such as namespace labels, service account annotations, or workload identity federation bindings.

## Rationale

Authorization decisions can consume identity metadata beyond a ServiceAccount name. An external identity can change where the provider explicitly trusts an annotation. Policy selection can change where the configured rules consume a namespace label. Verify the exact trust mapping or selector that gives a changed attribute its effect.

## Scope

Applies to organization-designated Kubernetes identity metadata used by authorization decisions, including ServiceAccount annotations and namespace labels. Covers clusters where federation or policy explicitly consumes those attributes. Projected service-account token audience issuance and validation are governed separately by SAF-K8S-0402-020.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: RBAC restrictions on service account and namespace metadata modification, admission policies that validate identity attribute changes against an approved registry, and audit logging of all identity attribute mutations.
- Implementation Surface: Protect and audit updates to the organization-designated ServiceAccount annotations and namespace labels consumed by deployed authorization policies. Restrict update/patch permissions, validate changes against an approved registry, and record the selected mutation events. For cloud federation and policy consumers, separately document and verify the actual provider trust mapping or selector and its resulting authorization effect.
- Owner Primary: Identity and Access Management
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Security Engineering

## Related Crosswalks

### NIST-800-53R5 AC-6

- Framework: NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0
- Requirement Title: Least Privilege
- Relation Type: supports
- Strength: moderate
- Applicability: conditional
- Strength Reason Code: partial-control-coverage
- Applicability Condition: Only for the organization-defined permissions governing mutation of the covered ServiceAccount and namespace identity metadata, where registrar update rights are narrowly assigned and routine workload identities lack those rights.

#### Framework Mapping Notes

The reference case grants a registrar update permission scoped to one ServiceAccount and one namespace, denies update attempts by an ordinary workload identity, and uses admission to constrain registrar changes to registered values. This supports least-privilege access for those exact metadata mutations only; it does not assess other permissions or establish least privilege for the workload identity overall.

### NIST-800-53R5 IA-4(9)

- Framework: NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 5.2.0
- Requirement Title: Attribute Maintenance and Protection
- Relation Type: supports
- Strength: moderate
- Applicability: conditional
- Strength Reason Code: partial-control-coverage
- Applicability Condition: Only where the organization designates a protected central identity-attribute registry as authoritative for the covered ServiceAccount annotations and namespace labels, and validates mutations against it before relying authorization decisions.

#### Framework Mapping Notes

The reference case maintains two synthetic identity attributes in a case-owned registry and demonstrates narrow registrar RBAC, admission denial of unapproved changes and removals, approved-value readback, and RequestResponse audit. This supports protection and maintenance of selected Kubernetes service identity attributes only. The namespaced fixture registry is not an organization-wide central store and does not prove that a cloud federation provider, NetworkPolicy, or other consumer maps or honors the attribute; establish those bindings and coverage separately.
