# SAF-K8S-0303-005 - MAC profile pre-enforcement audit-mode validation

- Domain: D03 - Workload and Pod Security
- Knowledge Area: 3.3 - Mandatory Access Controls
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Before promoting a governed custom workload profile or material revision, test the exact profile with representative workload paths, expected events and permitted operations using the mechanism supported by the target platform. Validate final enforcing behavior and recovery before production rollout.

## Objective

Identify unexpected decisions and observability gaps before promotion, and preserve workload behavior under the selected enforcing policy.

## Rationale

Seccomp logging actions, AppArmor complain and SELinux permissive operate at different boundaries and have platform-specific logging semantics. AppArmor can still enforce retained explicit or stacked denials. A clean observation interval establishes only the exercised paths and working event collection.

## Scope

Applies to governed custom seccomp, AppArmor and SELinux policies and material revisions on their actual supported kernel, runtime and workload configurations.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: A controlled profile staging and promotion process with direct evidence from the mechanism, workload, and audit collector.
- Implementation Surface: Record exact policy and platform versions, active mode and prerequisites, fixture paths and decision expectations, audit availability and exceptions. Exercise representative normal and infrequent paths, an attributable would-be policy event, final enforcing positive and negative checks, and rollback. Replace unsupported modes with a documented supported staging method.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Application Development teams
