# SAF-K8S-0405-022 - Tenant-scoped break-glass credential boundary enforcement

- Domain: D04 - Identity, Access, and Secrets Management
- Knowledge Area: 4.5 - Identity Abuse Detection and Mitigation
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: baseline

## Statement

Provision emergency access for the approved tenant, resource and operation only. Verify the effective issuer and authorization boundary and confirm the same credential is denied outside the authorized scope.

## Objective

Restore the approved tenant operation while preserving isolation and independent access in other environments.

## Rationale

The role and scope effective at the relying resource determine where the credential can be used. A kubeconfig context, credential name or identity selector does not by itself enforce tenant isolation.

## Scope

Applies to emergency privileges for the inventoried production tenants and shared services. Identify credential authority, resource and operation, real isolation boundary and inheritable privileges. Verify separate activation and assurance requirements under SAF-K8S-0405-021.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Effective credential acceptance and least-privilege authorization at the actual tenant and resource boundary.
- Implementation Surface: Test the approved resource, disallowed same-tenant operations, neighboring tenants and cluster-scoped effects. Review effective privileges, independent authorization, changes, exceptions and withdrawal across the governed paths.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; Identity and Access Management
