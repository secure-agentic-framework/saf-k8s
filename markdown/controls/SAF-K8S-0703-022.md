# SAF-K8S-0703-022 - GPU abuse pattern monitoring and detection for Kubernetes AI workloads

- Domain: D07 - Storage, Multi-tenancy, and Resource Governance
- Knowledge Area: 7.3 - Resource Governance and Priority
- Maturity: Practitioner
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Monitor governed accelerator workloads using documented allocation and device telemetry. Correlate notable signals with workload identity, owners, operating policy and approval context. Route unexplained activity or policy mismatches for adjudication and record the disposition and evidence quality.

## Objective

Surface actionable accelerator activity with enough evidence and context for an authorized reviewer to determine whether response is required.

## Rationale

Intensive accelerator use can be expected for approved work. Activity and workload metadata are triage signals. Reliable attribution and authorization review are needed to establish a policy violation, and incomplete context should remain visible as uncertainty.

## Scope

Applies to selected accelerator environments and their declared telemetry coverage. Document devices, collectors, workload identity mapping, retention, signal definitions and gaps for the deployed resource models.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Accelerator collection and workload attribution, review alert routing, and documented authorized adjudication.
- Implementation Surface: Verify telemetry freshness and coverage and use approved workload identity and policy sources. Evaluate meaningful signal windows, distinguish unknown context from conflict with known policy, and validate both approved high-activity and review-required cases. Retain alert routing and reviewer evidence and qualify any conclusions about cause or intent.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; ML Engineering
