# SAF-K8S-0910-029 - Centralized multi-cluster secret, certificate, and incident governance coordination

- Domain: D09 - AI Workload Security: Training, Serving, and Pipelines
- Knowledge Area: 9.10 - Multi-Cluster and Federated AI Security
- Maturity: Advanced
- Classification: ai-specific

## Statement

Coordinate approved secret lifecycle, certificate management and incident operations across the defined Kubernetes federation. Scope authority and actions to the intended targets, verify actual resulting state at each cluster, and retain unresolved or denied targets for reconciliation.

## Objective

Apply accountable centrally governed lifecycle and incident decisions consistently across the defined cluster set and verify their actual effect.

## Rationale

A central approval does not establish that each target cluster could execute it or reached the intended state. Cluster-specific identity boundaries, providers and permissions affect convergence.

## Scope

Applies to approved federation secret revisions, governed certificate distribution and incident coordination. Record issuer and cluster boundaries and actual mechanisms. Cryptographic key establishment, custody and unified compliance reporting are distinct requirements.

## Enforcement

- Primary Enforcement Point: Approved coordination record, authorized per-cluster operations and reconciled evidence of effective state and outstanding failures.
- Implementation Surface: Inventory cluster, lifecycle and incident authority. Exercise an approved change and a target that declines it. Verify exact per-cluster versions, leaf identity, affected authorization and independent access, disposition for unresolved targets, authorized retries and governed recovery. Evaluate actual certificate and key use separately.
- Owner Primary: Security Engineering
- Owner Supporting: Platform Engineering; GRC/Compliance; ML Platform Engineering
